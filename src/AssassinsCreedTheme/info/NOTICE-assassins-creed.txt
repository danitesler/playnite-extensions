Assassin's Creed Theme: notices

This is an unofficial fan theme. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Ubisoft Entertainment.
"Assassin's Creed" and related names and marks belong to Ubisoft; they are used here only to say what the look of
this theme is modeled on.

No Ubisoft artwork, logos, fonts, sounds or game files are included in this theme or were used to make it. The colors,
layout and motifs (a tab strip, diamond markers, corner brackets, hairline rules, a serif for headings) were drawn to
resemble the look of the games' menus.

The icons in Images/Icons and in Media.xaml (sources: icons/*.svg in the repository) and the add-on tile icon are
original artwork made for this theme, released under the repository's MIT license (LICENSE).

The theme bundles no fonts. It asks for fonts that ship with Windows (Segoe UI Semilight and Palatino Linotype, with
fallbacks); where one is missing, Windows substitutes another.

The Playnite Default theme files this theme starts from are MIT licensed: see LICENSE-Playnite.txt.
