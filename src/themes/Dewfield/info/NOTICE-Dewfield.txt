Dewfield — Unofficial Fan Theme Notice

Dewfield is an original, unofficial Playnite Desktop theme inspired by the warm wooden
ledger frames, honey-parchment menus, inventory slots, and cozy pastoral pixel craft
of Stardew Valley.

This theme is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or associated with
ConcernedApe LLC. It contains no official game artwork, sprites, logos, trademarks,
or proprietary fonts.

Original Assets & Open Fonts
- Custom vector Sprout-Star brand mark, bespoke pixel-crafted UI icon geometries,
  and procedural white-mask wood/parchment/glow sprites (art/sprites.py) are original
  works created for Dewfield.
