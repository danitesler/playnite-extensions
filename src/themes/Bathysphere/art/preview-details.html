Description

Descend past the lighthouse meridian into a flooded Art Deco metropolis of burnished brass, pressure-locked glass corridors, and forgotten grand ambitions — then ascend beyond the cloud line to a gilded 1912 exposition suspended in the sky.

Pair kinetic tonics and brasswork tools with improvised environmental traps, rewire security turrets on live analog needle gauges, and piece together the city's collapse from scattered reel-to-reel recordings and vellum dossiers.

“Constants and variables: a lighthouse on the rock, a bathysphere beneath the tide, and a city built where no city belongs.”
