Bathysphere is an unofficial fan theme inspired by the submerged Art Deco brass of Rapture and the gilded 1912 Beaux-Arts parchment and HUDs of Columbia across the BioShock franchise (BioShock, BioShock 2, and BioShock Infinite).
It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by 2K Games, Take-Two Interactive, Irrational Games, or Cloud Chamber.
No game assets, official logos, or proprietary font files are included.
