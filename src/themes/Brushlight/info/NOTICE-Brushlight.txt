Brushlight is an independent, unofficial Playnite desktop theme by Dani Tesler, inspired by the interface of Clair Obscur: Expedition 33. Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 is developed by Sandfall Interactive and published by Kepler Interactive. This theme is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Sandfall Interactive or Kepler Interactive.

The theme contains no files from the game. Its mark (a lozenge split by a hairline with two small nodes and a brush stroke), icons, paint sprites (brush stroke, ochre smear, dry-brush grain, banner dissolve, Art Deco lattice) and all other art were drawn or generated for this theme (art/icons.py, art/sprites.py); its colors follow the game's menus as they can be observed in public screenshots, and the game's art, icons, fonts and logo are not reproduced.

Fonts bundled with the theme in its Typography folder with their licenses (SIL Open Font License 1.1), trimmed to Latin and Cyrillic:
- Cinzel (Copyright 2020 The Cinzel Project Authors), titles and headings (OFL-Cinzel.txt). The game's own display face is not published; Cinzel stands in for it.
- Oswald (Copyright 2016 The Oswald Project Authors), numerals (OFL-Oswald.txt).
Body text uses Palatino Linotype, which ships with Windows and is not bundled.

The XAML files start from Playnite's Default theme (MIT); see LICENSE-Playnite.txt.
