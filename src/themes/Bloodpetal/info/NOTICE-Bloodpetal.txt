Bloodpetal

This is an unofficial fan-made theme, inspired by the interface of Diablo IV. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Blizzard Entertainment.
Diablo is a trademark or registered trademark of Blizzard Entertainment, Inc.

The theme contains no files from the game. Its mark, icons, frames, stone grain, haze, ember seam, glow and petal sprites were drawn or generated for this theme. Its colors follow the game's interface as it can be observed in public screenshots; the game's art, icons, fonts and logo are not reproduced.

No font is included. Headings use Palatino Linotype and body text Segoe UI, both of which ship with Windows.

The XAML files start from Playnite's Default theme (MIT); see LICENSE-Playnite.txt.
