About

Journey across the frozen meadow, / Light each lantern as you go.

Winterbloom is a quiet puzzle adventure painted on frosted paper. Carry a single flame through a sleeping valley, wake the lanterns one by one and follow the snow-lit paths to the orchard at the top of the hill.

Every screen is a hand-painted watercolour; every puzzle is a poem to be read aloud.
