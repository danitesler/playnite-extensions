Rimewash

This is an unofficial fan-made theme, inspired by the menu screens of Child of Light. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Ubisoft or the game's developers.
Child of Light and its logo are trademarks of their respective owners.

The theme contains no files from the game. Its mark (a hoarfrost crystal in a wobbled ring), icons, sprites and layout were drawn for this theme: the paper, plate, brush, chevron and cloud sprites are generated by art/sprites.py from noise, not traced. Colors and proportions follow the interface as it appears in publicly shared screenshots; no textures, fonts, sounds, logos or art are reproduced.

The type is Jost (The Jost Project Authors, SIL Open Font License 1.1) for body text, bundled in src/Typography with its license (OFL.txt); it stands in for the game's geometric sans. Titles use Constantia, which ships with Windows (not bundled).

The XAML files start from Playnite's Default theme (MIT); see LICENSE-Playnite.txt.
