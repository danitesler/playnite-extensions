Calling Card

This is an unofficial fan-made theme, inspired by the menus of Persona 5, Persona 5 Royal, Persona 5 Strikers and
Persona 5 Tactica. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by ATLUS or SEGA.
Persona is a trademark of ATLUS. ATLUS and SEGA are trademarks of SEGA Corporation.

The theme contains no files from the games. Its star mark, the slanted plates, the sawtooth rail edge and the halftone
dots were drawn for this theme; the icons are Heroicons (MIT, LICENSE-heroicons.txt).
Colors were measured on official store screenshots of the games; no texture, font or artwork is reproduced.

The games' lettering is custom artwork and is not included. Headings use Impact, the heavy condensed face that ships
with Windows (falling back to Haettenschweiler, then Arial Black); body text is Segoe UI.

The XAML files start from Playnite's Default theme (MIT); see LICENSE-Playnite.txt.
