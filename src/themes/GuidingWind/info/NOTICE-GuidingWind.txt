Guiding Wind

This is an unofficial fan-made theme inspired by the interface of Ghost of Tsushima. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Sucker Punch Productions LLC or Sony Interactive Entertainment LLC.

Ghost of Tsushima is a trademark or registered trademark of Sony Interactive Entertainment LLC.

The theme contains no assets from the game. Its mark (an original swirling wind-cloud kamon enclosing a tilted folded diamond seal), icons, Sayagata key-fret tile, dry-brush plate masks, and wind-wisp sprites were drawn or generated specifically for this theme. Its colors follow the game's menus and HUD as observed in public screenshots; the game's art, icons, fonts and logos are not reproduced.

Headings and buttons use Sora (Copyright 2019 The Sora Project Authors), an open font bundled with the theme. The font files and their license (SIL Open Font License 1.1, OFL.txt) are in the theme's Typography folder. Body text uses Segoe UI, which ships with Windows.

The XAML files start from Playnite's Default theme (MIT); see LICENSE-Playnite.txt.
