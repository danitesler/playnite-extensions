Stormrift

This is an unofficial fan-made theme, inspired by the interface of Heroes of the Storm. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Blizzard Entertainment, Inc.

Heroes of the Storm and Nexus are trademarks or registered trademarks of Blizzard Entertainment, Inc.

The theme contains no files from the game. Its mark (a faceted hexagon ring enclosing three inward-pointing storm shards), icons, hexagon lattice, vortex ring and glow sprites were drawn or generated for this theme. Its colors follow the game's menus and HUD as they can be observed in public screenshots; the game's art, icons, fonts and logo are not reproduced.

Headings use Sora (Copyright 2019 The Sora Project Authors), an open font bundled with the theme. The font files and their license (SIL Open Font License 1.1, OFL.txt) are in the theme's Typography folder. Body text uses Segoe UI, which ships with Windows.

The XAML files start from Playnite's Default theme (MIT); see LICENSE-Playnite.txt.
