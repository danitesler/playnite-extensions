## 3. Measured Proportions & Shapes
- **Title Medallions (`<◇ Card Gallery ◇>`)**: Centered dark charcoal/velvet capsule strip flanked at left and right by nested double rotated squares (`45°` diamonds, outer ~`14×14`, inner ~`8×8` gold outlined).
- **Primary Sapphire & Iron Action Buttons (`Back`, `Ready`, `€4.99`)**: Chamfered/notched hexagonal shoulders (~`6px` corner clip at `45°`) with outer `1.5px` burnished gold rim and top/bottom center cusp points (`ogee` notch), filled either with beveled diamond-lattice sapphire (`#2153ab`) or tonal obsidian fleur-de-lis damask (`#1e1e26`).
- **Beveled Hex Tabs (`armello-dlc`, `armello-overview-character`)**: Asymmetric chamfered tab crowns (`~8px` top corner cuts), inactive dims to muted brass icons, active outlines cleanly in `1.5px` bright gold `#c9a84c` + `#f2d878` over charcoal fill.
- **Deckled Illuminated Folio (`armello-thane`)**: Warm deckle-cut `#efe2b2` parchment with fine horizontal sepia hairlines (`1px`), hanging pointed heraldic pennant flags at top edge, dark bookmark tabs on the spine, and crisp mixed-case storybook typography.
- **Brass Scroll Rail (`armello-achievements`, `armello-dlc`)**: `1px` quiet gold spine wire centered in a calm gutter; slender elongated gold capsule thumb with three horizontal grip ticks.

## 4. Typography & Iconography Notes
- **Headers & Engraved Capitals (`HeadingFontFamily`)**: Armello uses a sculpted engraved Roman serif display face for screen medallions (`Card Gallery`, `Multiplayer`), CTA plaques (`Back`, `Open Chest`), and card headings. **Bundled font:** `Cinzel` (`Regular` & `Bold` static TTFs, SIL OFL 1.1, in `src/Typography/`).
- **Body, Metadata & Storybook Copy (`FontFamily`)**: Cards, achievement descriptions, and hero folios use a warm classical transitional serif for readable prose (`Georgia, Palatino Linotype, Segoe UI`, which renders cleanly on Windows Playnite via `Georgia` and identically in Chromium previews via `scripts/fonts/gelasio/` — exact `theme-fonts.mjs` match).
- **Icons**: Sharp Heraldic / Tabletop strokes & filled crests drawn directly as bespoke vector `Geometry` entries in `src/Media.xaml` (swords, shields, crowns, scrolls, magnifying glass with diamond finial, octagonal gear/sliders, notched chevrons) paired with standard Playnite `IconSize` scaling.
- **Assets Guarantee**: Zero copyrighted *Armello* or League of Geeks artwork, fonts, logos, or sprites are copied. Every texture and ornament sprite (`damask-tile.png`, `sapphire-quilt.png`, `plate-torn.png`, `filigree-corner.png`, `glow-frame.png`, `bloom.png`, `diamond-seam.png`, `checker-frieze.png`, `wyld-star.png`, `spirit-sweep.png`) is procedurally generated by `art/sprites.py` as white-mask RGBA PNGs tinted via Playnite ThemeModifier brushes.
