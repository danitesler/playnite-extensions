Wyldcrown is an unofficial, fan-made Playnite Desktop theme inspired by the heraldic tabletop interface of Armello.
It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by League of Geeks. "Armello" and its related names belong to their
owners and are named here strictly to describe the visual inspiration. Zero assets from the game are included: no captured
artwork, logos, fonts, textures, or audio.

All procedural ornament masks and textures (damask tile, sapphire diamond quilt, deckled parchment folio, corner filigree,
crown bloom and glow frames, diamond seams, checker frieze, Wyld Star emblem, and spirit sweep) are original white-mask
RGBA PNGs generated by art/sprites.py in this repository. All vector icons are original stroked glyphs drawn in
src/Media.xaml, and color tokens in src/tokens.css are calibrated from public UI reference captures for WCAG legibility.

Playnite's Default Desktop theme templates serve as the structural base for the XAML (MIT; see LICENSE-Playnite.txt).
