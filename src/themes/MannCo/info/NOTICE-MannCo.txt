Mann Co

This is an unofficial fan-made theme, inspired by the menus of Team Fortress 2. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Valve Corporation.
Team Fortress, Team Fortress 2, RED, BLU and Mann Co. are trademarks and/or registered trademarks of Valve Corporation.

The theme contains no files from the game. Its mark and every icon were drawn for this theme. Colors follow the values of the game's own interface scheme as published in the default HUD resource files; the game's textures, bitmaps and fonts are not reproduced or included.

The game's UI fonts (TF2 Build, TF2 Secondary) are proprietary and are not included. The theme uses Verdana and Trebuchet MS, which Valve's own scheme lists as fallbacks and which ship with Windows.

The XAML files start from Playnite's Default theme (MIT); see LICENSE-Playnite.txt.
