Backbeat

This is an unofficial fan-made theme, inspired by the comic-book, cel-shaded look of Hi-Fi RUSH. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Tango Gameworks, Bethesda Softworks, ZeniMax Media, KRAFTON, Inc. or any other owner of that game.
Hi-Fi RUSH is a trademark of its respective owner.

The theme contains no files from the game. Its mark, icons, halftone and speed-line sprites, speech balloon and layout were drawn or generated for this theme. No character, logo, font or artwork from the game is reproduced.
A few colors follow the game's palette as it can be observed in public screenshots.

The theme names Impact and Segoe UI in its font lists; both come with Windows and no font is included.

The XAML files start from Playnite's Default theme (MIT); see LICENSE-Playnite.txt.
