Hexfall

This is an unofficial fan-made theme, inspired by the interface of Age of Wonders: Planetfall. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Triumph Studios or Paradox Interactive.

Age of Wonders and Age of Wonders: Planetfall are trademarks or registered trademarks of Triumph Studios B.V. and Paradox Interactive AB.

The theme contains no files from the game. Its mark (a hexagon ring with a shard dropping out of the gap), icons, hexagon lattice, radar and glow sprites were drawn or generated for this theme. Its colors follow the game's menus as they can be observed in public screenshots; the game's art, icons, fonts and logo are not reproduced.

Headings use Sora (Copyright 2019 The Sora Project Authors), an open font bundled with the theme. The font files and their license (SIL Open Font License 1.1, OFL.txt) are in the theme's Typography folder. Body text uses Segoe UI, which ships with Windows.

The XAML files start from Playnite's Default theme (MIT); see LICENSE-Playnite.txt.
