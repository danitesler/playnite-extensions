Testbed — Unofficial Fan Theme Notice

Testbed is an original, unofficial Playnite Desktop theme inspired by the modular
cleanroom test-chamber architecture, stepped Aperture panels, antline indicator
dots, and Portal Blue / Propulsion Orange polarity palette of Portal 2.

This theme is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or associated with
Valve Corporation. It contains no official game artwork, logos, trademarks, or
proprietary fonts.
