Rumpus is an unofficial, fan-made Playnite theme inspired by the menu interface of Party Animals.

It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Recreate Games, the game's publisher, or any rights holder of
Party Animals. All names and trademarks belong to their respective owners and appear only to identify the inspiration.

This package contains no game art, logo, font or screen capture from the game. Colours were measured from public
screenshots; every shape (the lip pills, the bone, the paw mark, the chalkboard note, the confetti) is original and
drawn in XAML.

Text is set in Nunito (Nunito Project Authors) and the handwriting in Patrick Hand (Patrick Wagesreiter), both open
fonts bundled with the theme. The font files and their licenses (SIL Open Font License 1.1) are in the theme's
Typography folder; copies are in LICENSE-nunito.txt and LICENSE-patrickhand.txt.

Icons are from Lucide (ISC License), see LICENSE-lucide.txt.
