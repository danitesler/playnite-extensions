Vice Dusk is an unofficial fan theme inspired by the Vice sunset magenta-plum twilight gradients, neon flamingo highlights, coastal palm silhouettes, stark white-to-fade selection bars, and pill toggles of Grand Theft Auto VI's pause, exploration, progress, dossier, and settings screens.
It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive Software, Inc.
No game assets, official logos, copyrighted artwork, or proprietary font files are included.
