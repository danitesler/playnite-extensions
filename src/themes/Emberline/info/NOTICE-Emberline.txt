Emberline

This is an unofficial fan-made theme, inspired by the menus of Gears 5. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by The Coalition or Microsoft.
Gears 5 and Gears of War are trademarks or registered trademarks of Microsoft Corporation.

The theme contains no files from the game. Its mark (a broken cog-tooth ring), icons, burning frame, flare, glow, scratches and frost sprites were drawn or generated for this theme. Its colors follow the game's menus as they can be observed in public screenshots; the game's art, icons, fonts and logo are not reproduced.

Headings use Russo One (by Jovanny Lemonad), an open font bundled with the theme. The font file and its license (SIL Open Font License 1.1, OFL.txt) are in the theme's Typography folder. Body text uses Bahnschrift, which ships with Windows.

The XAML files start from Playnite's Default theme (MIT); see LICENSE-Playnite.txt.
