Starlane — Fan-Made Theme Notice

Starlane is an unofficial, fan-made desktop theme for Playnite inspired by the cassette-futurist graphic design, starfighter targeting displays, and holographic interfaces of classic space-opera cinema.

Starlane is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Lucasfilm Ltd. LLC or The Walt Disney Company. No official logos, copyrighted artwork, film stills, or proprietary fonts are included in this theme. All vector icons, procedural texture masks, and branding geometry are original creations authored for this theme.
