## Sampling method

The captures were drawn into a canvas in the browser and read with `getImageData`: for each region, the brightest pixel, the mean, and the most common colours above a luminance floor (8-level buckets). Region coordinates are in the 1728x1080 image. Text and rims are anti-aliased, so the mode is used for fills and the upper modes for text.

## Interface rules observed

- **Palette.** Two colours do all the work on a warm black, scanlined backdrop: amber/orange for frames, text and buttons, lime for titles, the selected tab and the selected option. Grey steel marks disabled rows; teal and yellow are dialogue only.
- **Titles.** Screen titles ("Options", "Codex", "Squad Selection", "Research") are lime, in a wide square display face (Korataki), top-left, mixed case, with no rule under them. Subtitles ("Upgrade your equipment") are small lime body text.
- **Menu lists.** Left-aligned body text about 20px apart at 1080p, no bullets. The selected option is a full-width olive plate with a 1px lime rim whose right end is cut at an angle; its label turns lime. Codex lists add a small orange "+" cross on the selected row's left edge and a lime "New" tag before each entry.
- **Panels.** Description and codex panels are warm black plates in a 1-2px copper rim with a soft amber bloom, corners cut at 45 degrees (the long description box has angled "<  >" ends). Image panels sit in a brighter orange frame.
- **Buttons.** Bottom bar buttons (Info, Exit, Reset to Defaults, Mark All Viewed, Back) are dark amber plates about 40px tall at 1080p with the top-left and bottom-right corners cut, a 1px amber rim, a faint honeycomb texture and gold text centred.
- **Research rows.** Large parallelogram plates leaning right, honeycomb fill, bright amber rim; the focused one is brighter, a disabled one is grey steel.
- **Tabs.** The codex tab strip is a lime rule with the selected tab as a lit lime trapezoid standing on it; the idle tab is dim olive text.
- **Scroll.** A thin lime-olive rail with a small amber block as the thumb.
- **Texture.** Fine horizontal scanlines (about 3px pitch at 1080p) over the backdrops and image panels; honeycomb inside plates.
- **Squad selection.** A vertical lime light beam marks the selected squad member.

## Motion

Observed in play and in the capture sequence (no frame-stepped video was available, so durations are by eye):

- Menu focus changes are near instant: the selection plate snaps to the new row while its rim and glow fade up over about 0.1-0.15s.
- Buttons brighten on focus (fill and rim) rather than moving.
- Lit panels and the squad beam pulse very slowly; the HUD's targeting brackets close in on a target when it is locked.

Relay's choices from that: 0.12s hover fades on buttons, rows and tabs; Play's bloom swells and a light band sweeps its honeycomb while hovered; covers get lime targeting brackets that close in (0.18s, eased); the empty page is a codex panel with a breathing mass relay mark and a scan line.
