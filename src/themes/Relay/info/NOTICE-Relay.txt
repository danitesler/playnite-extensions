Relay is an unofficial, fan-made Playnite theme inspired by the interface of Mass Effect 2.

It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by BioWare, Electronic Arts or any rights holder of Mass Effect.
All names and trademarks belong to their respective owners and appear only to identify the inspiration.

This package contains no game art, logo, font or screen capture from Mass Effect. Every icon, plate shape, glow
sprite and the tile mark is original, drawn or generated by the theme's own scripts.

Headings use Orbitron (by Matt McInerney), an open font bundled with the theme. The font file and its license
(SIL Open Font License 1.1, OFL.txt) are in the theme's Typography folder.
