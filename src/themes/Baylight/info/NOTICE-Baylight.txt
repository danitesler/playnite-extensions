Baylight 27 is an unofficial, fan-made Playnite theme inspired by the look of macOS 27 ("Golden Gate") in its Dark appearance.
It is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Apple Inc. macOS, Liquid Glass, Siri, SF Pro and SF Symbols are trademarks
of Apple Inc., used here only to describe what the theme is inspired by.

No Apple assets are included:
- No Apple fonts. Text uses Segoe UI Variable, which ships with Windows 11 (Segoe UI on Windows 10); nothing is bundled.
- No SF Symbols. Every icon and the 24 menu item icons were drawn for this theme (art/icons.ps1, art/menu-icons.json).
- No Apple wallpaper. The silk desktop picture is an original procedural image (art/sprites.py); only its colours were sampled
  from Apple's public macOS 27 design resources page.
- No Apple logo. The Baylight 27 mark (a squircle holding a sun over two swells) is original.
