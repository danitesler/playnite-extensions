Slipstream is an unofficial, fan-made Playnite theme inspired by the menu interface of Star Wars: Galactic Racer.

It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Lucasfilm Ltd., The Walt Disney Company, the game's
developer or publisher, or any rights holder of Star Wars. All names and trademarks belong to their respective
owners and appear only to identify the inspiration.

This package contains no game art, logo, font or screen capture from the game. Colours were measured from public
screenshots; every shape (pills, stripes, the orbit plot, the tile mark) is original and drawn in XAML.

Headings use Unbounded, an open font bundled with the theme. The font files and their license (SIL Open Font
License 1.1, OFL.txt) are in the theme's Typography folder; a copy is in LICENSE-unbounded.txt.

Icons are from Lucide (ISC License), see LICENSE-lucide.txt.
