Stride — Unofficial Fan Theme Notice

Stride is an original, unofficial Playnite Desktop theme inspired by the blinding
snow-white Swiss architectural aesthetic, hairline grid systems, Runner Vision
crimson accents, and diagonal momentum geometry of Mirror's Edge.

This theme is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or associated with
Electronic Arts Inc. or EA Digital Illusions CE AB (DICE). It contains no
official game artwork, logos, trademarks, or proprietary fonts.
