Henchwork is an unofficial fan-made desktop theme for Playnite inspired by the
user interface of Evil Genius 2: World Domination.

It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Rebellion Developments
or any other rights holder. All trademarks, game names, and related marks
belong to their respective owners.

No game logos, artwork, icons, fonts, sound effects, or other game assets are
included in this theme. The vault-eye mark, all vector pictograms, and the
procedural masks are original works created for this theme.
