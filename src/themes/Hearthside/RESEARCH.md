## Observed material hierarchy (`id=628`)

1. **Carved Oak & Tavern Timber** (`#170e09` night shadow → `#24150c` box rim → `#311d11` board planks → `#412817` raised panels → `#5b3b23` resting wood plates → `#7c5432` lit selected planks): forms the outer window rim, sidebar pillars, top header beam, game list backing, and wooden button plaques. Always warm dark umber with woodgrain fiber, never flat charcoal.
2. **Riveted Wrought Iron & Slate** (`#222329` dark recess → `#35373f` nameplate fill → `#5e616e` top specular edge): chamfered octagonal nameplates (`Options`, `Card Crafting`, `Choose a Mode`, `Book of Heroes`) flanked by twin round brass/iron rivets and a thin inner gold trim line.
3. **Aged Warm Parchment** (`#ebd9b4` center, `#f6ead0` highlight, `#b99b6b` worn deckled edge, `#2b1a0e` espresso ink, `#4e260c` title ink, `#6d4a26` caption ink): the Collection Manager open book, Card Crafting info scroll, Quest cards, and tooltips. Provides dramatic light-on-dark material contrast against the tavern timber while keeping dense text effortless to read.
4. **Burnished Gold & Aged Brass** (`#6b4319` deep groove → `#8c6226` resting edge → `#c9963b` brass clasps & frames → `#efc342` gold selection → `#ffe270` lantern highlight): corner L-brackets, riveted frame borders, active tabs, selected deck borders, and scrollbar diamond thumbs.
5. **Arcane Mana Blue, Playable Emerald & Crimson Wax**:
   - **Mana Blue** (`#1d4e8f` → `#4aa3f0` → `#9ce2ff` / `#00b8ff` bloom): Main Menu compass medallion core, mana tray sliders/progress bars, and active/running game mana crystals.
   - **End Turn Gold & Playable Emerald** (`#f6c41c` → `#ffe566` with `#331c06` ink; `#2ee655` hover aura): the primary Play CTA button and keyboard focus ring.
   - **Health / Wax-Seal Crimson** (`#c82a25` / `#ff5447`): notification wax pips, red ribbon bookmark tabs, danger/close hovers, and low-score badges.
   - **Legendary Amber** (`#ff8c14`): filter-active diamond gem and update alerts.

## Silhouette & lighting rules

- **Top-down lantern lighting**: every beveled plank, nameplate, and frame has a bright warm highlight along its top lip (`BevelLightBrush`) and a deep carved shadow under its bottom edge (`BevelShadowBrush`).
- **Hardware details**: twin round rivets on chamfered iron nameplates and primary plank buttons; 4-corner brass clasps on hero frames and cards; faceted diamond gems on dividers, sliders, filter-active badges, and running indicators.
- **Calm reading surfaces**: game list rows stay unboxed at rest on dark oak planks; dense metadata sits on clean high-contrast parchment with sepia dividers and dark carved-wood tag tokens.
