Description

Pull up a chair by the fire. Emberfall Tavern is a cozy card battler where every duel is played on a hand-carved board, every deck is a story, and the innkeeper always has one more trick up his sleeve.

Collect over 400 cards across nine classes, build decks in the Collection Manager, and climb the ladder from Copper to Legend.
