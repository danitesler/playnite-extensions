## Measured visual anatomy

- **Value ladder & accent budget (canvas-sampled):** paper/plates `#F7F7F7`–`#F8F8F8` (neutral, not warm) -> segment grey `#A9A9A9` -> graphite numerals `#7A7A7A` -> slate `#6A8996` -> teal ground `#193A3B` (dominant on Quest/System, shadows `#142829` / `#092728`) -> oxblood ground `#380605` / `#691817` -> ink `#060A0D` / dialog `#1B1B1B`. Accent brush per menu: crimson `#E43457` (player menu, shop, dialogue: the most frequent), deep crimson `#A6233C`–`#C42B48` (load), teal `#22A7AD` / `#169CA3` (system, settings), orange `#DB6522`, yellow `#D5B601` (quest), blue `#017AC8` (shop splat), magenta `#AB2879` (party). Area: ~70–80% painted dark ground, ~12–18% white type, rules and plates, ~6–12% brush accent, and the accent sits almost only on the current item.
- **Material & edge anatomy:** the selection is never a rectangle: a horizontal **dry-brush band** with ragged top and bottom edges, bristle streaks, a feathered start and a splattered end, droplets around it, sometimes a second colour flick behind (mint behind crimson). Grounds are painterly ink washes (mottled, splattered, scraped). Every other line is a crisp **white hairline** (2px at 1080p ≈ 1px at 720p): row underlines that run past the text, doubled rules around slots, vertical rules, long diagonal strokes and thin **concentric compass rings**. Plates (`MONEY`, prompts, tags, weekday) are flat `#F7F7F7` with a **slanted left end** (~20°) or a pointed notch, no radius, no shadow. Description cards are off-white with a torn / brushed right edge. No glows, no drop shadows, no gradients on chrome.
- **Typography voice:** display = wide, heavy, high-contrast Didone set as **caps with an enlarged initial** (`SKILL` = big S + small caps), white on dark or black on brush; huge section titles cropped by the frame (`SYSTEM`, `QUEST`, `ITEM` at ~200px, grey or white). Numerals are the same Didone, wide and tabular-feeling (`341,575`, `09/24`, `038:34`), with **ghost leading zeros** in a muted tone. Body / list text = a transitional serif with moderate contrast (`Medicinal Herb`, `A blow dart dipped…`), sentence case, white; captions in tiny Didone caps (`MONEY`, `TITLE MENU`). Stand-ins: **Playfair Display** (OFL, Bold/Black) for display and numerals, **Gelasio** (OFL, Georgia-metric) for body.
- **Proportions & negative space:** list rows ~75px at 1080p (≈ 48–50px at 720p) in the menus, 2px rules; generous left inset, rows often start after a vertical hairline; the right third is usually art. Dense info is grouped in plates, not boxes.
- **Observed UI parts & state transitions:** idle row = white text on its hairline; current = brush band wipes in from the left (the band arrives with a slight overshoot, splatter appears at the tail), text may flip to black on bright brush (player menu, dialogue) or stay white (shop); a subtitle/caption appears under the current item (`AWAKEN STEEL`, `Resume from saved data`). Category tabs: inverted white triangles with ink icons, the current one larger with a caption below. Prompts: circle button glyphs on slanted white plates.
