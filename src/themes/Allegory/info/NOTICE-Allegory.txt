Allegory is an independent, unofficial Playnite desktop theme by Dani Tesler, inspired by the menus of Metaphor: ReFantazio. Metaphor: ReFantazio and its name are the property of ATLUS and SEGA; this theme is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by them.

The theme contains no files from the game. Its name, its mark (two compass rings crossed by a diagonal hairline around a quill-nib spire), its icons, the dry-brush, splatter, torn-edge and painted-ground masks (art/sprites.py) and the banner edge were drawn for this theme from simple shapes and procedural noise. No logo, logotype, character art, screenshot, sound or font from the game is reproduced. Its colors follow the game's menu palette as seen in publicly shared screenshots (see RESEARCH.md).

The game's own typefaces are not used or bundled; Playfair Display SC stands in for its wide Didone menu type.

Fonts bundled with the theme in its Typography folder with their licenses (SIL Open Font License 1.1):
- Playfair Display SC (Copyright 2017 The Playfair Display Project Authors, Reserved Font Name "Playfair Display"), Bold and Black, Google Fonts static cuts shipped unmodified: headings, titles, Play, numerals and captions (OFL-PlayfairDisplaySC.txt).
- Gelasio (Copyright 2022 The Gelasio Project Authors), Regular and Bold: body text (OFL-Gelasio.txt).

The XAML files start from Playnite's Default theme (MIT); see LICENSE-Playnite.txt.
