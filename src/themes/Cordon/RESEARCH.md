# Cordon — research & sources

Unofficial fan theme. It reproduces the *look* of the menus of **S.T.A.L.K.E.R.: Shadow of Chernobyl** (GSC Game World, 2007), mainly the Options screen, from a capture and from the game's own UI configuration. No GSC Game World textures, fonts, logos or other assets are used or shipped: the rust and grain are procedural (`art/textures.py`), the add-on mark is an original radiation trefoil (`art/mark.svg`), the icons are Phosphor (MIT). See `info/NOTICE-Cordon.txt`.

There is no published design system; the "tokens" (`src/tokens.css`) are a hand-made table. Each value there is tagged `[q]` quoted from game config or engine source, `[s]` sampled from a capture or texture, or `[e]` estimated.

## Chosen values

- Surfaces, metal, text and accent tokens, with their tags, are the table in `src/tokens.css` (and `AGENTS.md` -> Tokens for which Playnite key plays each one).
- Shape: square plates (`--zone-radius` 0, `--zone-radius-sm` 1 for the scroll thumb); the frame and plate edges are drawn, never rounded.
- Type: Letterica (body) and Graffiti1 / Graffiti (headings) first, then Bahnschrift SemiCondensed, Arial Narrow, Tahoma. Sizes 12 / 14 / 16 / 20 / 28 mapped from letterica16 (14), graffiti19 (16), letterica25 / graffiti22 (20) and graffiti32 (28) at 1024x768.
- Chrome and selection: worn steel plates for the rail, caption band, tabs and buttons (`BevelTemplate`: rust overlay, shade, lit top line, dark bottom line); engine selection bar (warm brown, no outline); a square lamp indicator for check boxes and a dim dropdown chevron for combo boxes; broken-line section rule.

## Design decisions

- Dark only; the game has no light variant.
- Clear Sky shares most of the color table; Call of Pripyat went neutral grey, so it is not the reference.
- The frame is a flat steel brush with a procedural rust overlay instead of the game's painted texture with cables, pipes and bolts (see `AGENTS.md` -> Deviations).
- Library art keeps its color; the game's menus sit over a desaturated scene, which is not reproduced.

## Not done / not verified

- No capture of the real game was available to this repo beyond the one screenshot listed above; the `[e]` tokens in `src/tokens.css` are estimates, not measurements (plate hover and pressed, rivet, bevel light, panel-deep, field-hover, void).
- The frame atlas values (`ui_hud.dds` / `ui_pda.dds`) come from a mirror that may be modded.
- Nothing has been checked in a running Playnite (built and validated on Linux).
