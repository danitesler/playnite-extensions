# Cordon — theme notes

## What this is

Playnite **Desktop** theme (`ThemeApiVersion` 2.9.0) inspired by the menus of **S.T.A.L.K.E.R.: Shadow of Chernobyl** (GSC Game World, 2007), mainly its Options screen. Clear Sky shares most of the color table; Call of Pripyat went neutral grey, so it is not the reference. The look: grainy olive-black panels inside a rusted, riveted steel frame, worn metal plates for tabs and buttons, near-black value fields with a grey hairline and a red-lens dropdown knob, amber lamp toggles, beige labels and cream headings. Dark only.

**Unofficial fan theme.** No GSC assets: the rust and grain are procedural (`art/textures.py`), the mark is an original trefoil, the icons are Phosphor. The game's bitmap fonts (Letterica, Graffiti) are not shipped; the theme names them first and falls back to Bahnschrift SemiCondensed, Arial Narrow and Tahoma. S.T.A.L.K.E.R. is a trademark of GSC Game World (`info/NOTICE-Cordon.txt`).

Shared anatomy (file map, shell rules, game page skeleton, metadata pane, build and first-run checks): **`../AGENTS.md`**. Loading rules and build checks: **`.claude/skills/playnite-theme-dev/reference.md`**.

## Component spacing (`src/Common.xaml`)

1080p measurements scaled by 2/3: buttons 30 tall (`ButtonPadding` 18,6), fields 28 (`InputPadding` 8,5), menu rows 28 (`MenuItemPadding` 10,5,16,5 in `MenuPadding` 4), list rows 26 (`ListBoxItemPadding` 8,4), group header 22px with 8 above and 6 below, tooltips 8,5, `IconSize` 18, `GameBannerHeight` 340, details pane 340, grid pane 280.

`BevelTemplate` (Common.xaml) is the plate every steel surface uses: the host's Background, the rust overlay (`Images/rust.png`, tiled at its own size), a shade toward the bottom (`BevelShadowBrush` through a gradient mask), a lit top line, a dark bottom line and a 1px edge in the host's BorderBrush.

## Game page

Skeleton and metadata pane as in `../AGENTS.md`: Steam screenshots in the left column above the description, banner spacer scaled with `HeroArt` through `MathConverter` (100/340 details, 72/340 grid). Differences: section headings are cream over the game's broken line (dashed 1px `InputBorderBrush`), no box; the title is cream with 240px of broken line; the metadata pane and notes are inset wells inside the grey hairline; Play is the lit plate with an amber lamp left of its label.

## Deviations

- The game's frame is a painted texture with cables, pipes and bolts; here the frame is a flat steel brush with a procedural rust overlay, a seam and single rivets. ThemeModifier recolors the brushes under the textures, not the textures themselves.
- The lamp halo is a radial gradient, not a blur (no effects on controls).
- Hover text on plates is the game's dark ink; the game also brightens the button texture, which here is a lighter brush.
- Library art keeps its color (the game's menus sit over a desaturated scene).
- Fonts are fallbacks unless TrueType conversions of the game's fonts are installed; WPF may not resolve `Bahnschrift SemiCondensed` as a family name, in which case the regular width of Bahnschrift is used.
- Section captions are sentence case; WPF `TextBlock` has no casing property, so the game's ALL CAPS window caption is not reproduced.

## Previews

`art/preview-details.src.html` and `art/preview-settings.src.html` are approximate HTML replicas (same tokens, sizes, textures and shell). `python3 art/previews.py` inlines the Phosphor icons into `preview-*.html`; `.\scripts\take-screenshots.ps1 -Extension cordon` renders `art/details.png` and `art/settings.png`. They are not Playnite captures. The settings preview reuses the Options screen's own section and row names as a nod to the reference.
