Visorline
This is an unofficial fan-made theme, inspired by the menus and HUD of Crysis 2 as seen in public screenshots. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Crytek or Electronic Arts.
Crysis and Crysis 2 are trademarks or registered trademarks of Crytek GmbH. Nanosuit and CryNet Systems are names from that game.
The theme contains no files from the game. Its mark (a hex cell broken by a visor slit), icons, honeycomb, grain, scanline, segment and bloom sprites were drawn or generated for this theme. Its colors follow the game's menus and HUD as they can be observed in public screenshots; the game's art, icons, fonts and logos are not reproduced.
Titles and labels use Rajdhani (by Indian Type Foundry), an open font bundled with the theme in Bold, trimmed to Latin and Cyrillic. The font file and its license (SIL Open Font License 1.1, OFL.txt) are in the theme's Typography folder. Body text uses Bahnschrift, which ships with Windows.
The XAML files start from Playnite's Default theme (MIT); see LICENSE-Playnite.txt.
