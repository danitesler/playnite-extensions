## Measured Palette & Value Ladder

Pixel-sampled from the 1024×576 captures via Python Pillow/NumPy:

1. **Deepest Void / Obsidian Frame (`#060505` -> adapted to `#081113` / `#0C191B`)**: Screen corners, rail, top bar, dialog title band, sunken wells. Always carries a cool petrol-ink undertone rather than neutral grey charcoal.
2. **Primary Painted Ground — Deep Petrol-Teal (`#153637` / `#1F2B29` / `#274D4F` -> adapted to `#102224` canvas, `#152C2F` elevated ledger, `#1C383B` resting controls)**: The unmistakable cool verdigris-petrol canvas that anchors `Settings`, `Equipment`, `Memorandum`, `Quest`, and `Load` (85%+ of visual area stays dark, calm, and legible).
3. **Atmospheric Shadow Wash — Deep Burgundy-Crimson (`#391213` / `#421010` / `#51161B` -> `#421119`)**: Subtle right-edge / anchor contrast wash behind focal panels, creating Metaphor's signature cool-teal-vs-warm-burgundy depth.
4. **Stark Chalk-Ivory Paper (`#F2F2F2` / `#F8F3F3` / `#FCFCFC` -> `#F4F1EA` with `#11181A` pitch ink)**: High-contrast paper slips for active tabs (`Nature`), active segmented options (`ON`, `High`, `120`), metadata chips (`Search`, `Effect`), torn-edge tooltips (`Fermis Ointment`), `NO.01` score plaques, and primary `OK` buttons.
5. **Measured Expressive Gouache Accents (5–15% total area budget)**:
   - **Carmine Rose-Crimson (`#E03254` / `#E0375A` / `#D93151` -> `#C92446` base [5.49:1 white text], `#D92B4E` hover [4.77:1])**: Hero Play CTA brushstroke, destructive Exit row, subtle active rail pip.
   - **Verdigris Cyan-Teal (`#22A8AF` / `#01AFB6` -> `#117E86` solid brush fill [4.82:1 white text], `#22B5BD` bright border/gauge highlight)**: Selected rows, Settings tree selection brush, popup menu highlight, sliders, focus accents.
   - **Royal Quest Gold (`#CDB000` / `#A99119` -> `#D6A618` [8.06:1 pitch ink])**: Running/launching game indicator sweeps, stepping Quest Rank diamonds (`◇ ◆ ◇`), and running Play plate.
   - **Memorandum Emerald (`#18A15F` / `#46AE87` -> `#24BC70` [7.26:1 on top bar])**: Active filter diamond pip (`DataChangeNotifBrush`) and positive rating accents.

## Material & Edge Anatomy

- **Zero bubbly SaaS rounding**: Controls are razor-sharp rectilinear plates (`0px`–`2px` corners) contrasted violently against **organic hand-painted gouache brushstroke edges** and **hand-torn paper slips**.
- **Distressed Chalk-White Painted Spine (`2–3px`)**: Every menu splits left navigation/index from content via a hand-painted vertical chalk seam with subtle dry-brush breaks (`02_settings`, `03_inventory`, `07_load`, `10_equipment`, `11_memorandum`, `12_quest`).
- **Architectural Drafting Compass Geometry**: Ultra-fine `1px` translucent white circles, tangent diagonal rays, and crosshair intersections float delicately over anchor zones (`01_title`, `02_settings`, `04_codex`, `06_modal`, `13_status`) — never boxy sci-fi corner brackets.
- **No blurred drop shadows**: Depth comes purely from stark value contrast (chalk ivory on deep petrol-obsidian), distressed ink hairlines, and layered painterly washes.
- **Scrollbar anatomy (`07_load`, `11_memorandum`)**: Razor-slim pure chalk-white vertical thumb sliding along an ultra-thin hairline ruler track with tiny top/bottom serif cap ticks.

## Typography Voice

- **Display / Headings (`SETTINGS`, `LOAD`, `MEMORANDUM`, `EQUIPMENT`, `SYSTEM`, `OK`, `MISSION`)**: Monumental, high-contrast Renaissance uppercase serif — bundled **`Cinzel`** (`Cinzel-Regular.ttf`, `Cinzel-Bold.ttf`, SIL OFL-1.1 from `scripts/fonts/cinzel/static/`) in `src/Typography/`, referenced as `./Typography/#Cinzel, Cinzel, Georgia`.
- **Body, Lists & Descriptions (`Screen Brightness`, `Man-Eater's Grotto`, `A speciality of St. Fermis Church.`)**: Sharp transitional editorial serif — Windows **`Georgia`** (mapped to OFL-1.1 **`Gelasio`** in `scripts/data/fonts.json`, zero system-font drift in Chromium previews), `14px` base with crisp contrast and tabular rhythm.
- **Monospace**: `Consolas` (mapped to `Cascadia Mono` in previews).
