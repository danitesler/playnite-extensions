Arcfolio is an unofficial community Playnite Desktop theme inspired by the
painted Renaissance manuscript, architectural drafting arc, and expressive
gouache menu aesthetic of Metaphor: ReFantazio.

It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by ATLUS, Studio Zero, or
SEGA. All artwork, vector marks, procedural brush/texture masks, icons, and
XAML templates in this package are original creations built specifically for
Playnite.

Typography:
- Cinzel (Regular, Bold) — SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL.txt bundled under
  Typography/), subset to Latin and Cyrillic.
- Georgia / Palatino Linotype / Consolas — Windows system fonts.
