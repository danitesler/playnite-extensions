# Blacklist — research & sources

Unofficial fan theme. It reproduces the *look* of the menus of **Need for Speed: Most Wanted (2005)** (EA Black Box, PC) from public screenshots. No EA files, logos, fonts or textures are used or shipped: the mark, the hazard stripes and the corner brackets are drawn for this theme (`art/mark.svg`, `src/Common.xaml`), the icons are Phosphor (`art/icons.py`). Notice: `info/NOTICE-Blacklist.txt`.

The game has no published design system. Everything below was **observed from captures**; the values were sampled when the theme was first built and were not re-sampled for this write-up. Where the earlier notes do not record the evidence (frame ids, exact URLs) this file says so instead of filling the gap.

### Frontend skin

Body text `#DDE1CC`, values `#FCFCFC`, hints `#ADB091`, controls cells `#242424`, selected cell `#34240C`, carousel chevrons `#CE9E22`, car stat bars `#80C0E4`.

### Accents

Rap Sheet lime `#A4E245`, heat yellow `#FCCC48`, redline about `#D85038`, Blacklist rank tag `#D49818`.

### Shape and size

Square corners everywhere; the only rounding is the 2px key glyph boxes of the prompt bar. L brackets: 4px stroke, 16px legs at 1080p (3 and 10 here). Hazard stripes: 45 degrees, 33px period at 1080p (20 here), half black, half amber-brown. List rows 48px at 1080p (32 here); prompt bar 52px (35).

## Design decisions

- **Two skins, mixed**: the amber skin gives the panels, bands, stripes and brackets; the frontend skin gives the black input cells and the blackletter title.
- **Selection** is the game's: amber L brackets around icons, tabs and buttons, the dark select fill with a 3px amber bar on rows. The solid amber plate is reserved for the default and pressed button (the GPS dialog's Yes) and the Play button.
- **Layout** per the repo's shell rules: 44px compact rail on the left, 56px header band closed by a 6px hazard band.
- **Signature motifs** (drawn in the previews): hazard stripes, amber L brackets, the 3px selected-row bar, the blackletter game title over a short stripe, the amber plate with dark ink.

## Not done / gaps

- No frame ids or URLs for the frontend video frames; the amber-skin wiki file names are the only exact references. Re-sample against fresh captures if exact digits matter.
- The font identification (Eurostile, blackletter) is from forum notes and by eye; no font file was compared.
- The game's textures (grunge, ink splatter, olive/sepia grading of the city) are deliberately not reproduced.
- The theme ships no fonts; Eurostile and Old English Text MT are used when installed.
