Blacklist

This is an unofficial fan-made theme, inspired by the menus of Need for Speed: Most Wanted (2005). It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Electronic Arts or EA Black Box.
Need for Speed and Need for Speed: Most Wanted are trademarks of Electronic Arts Inc.

The theme contains no files from the game. Its mark, the hazard stripes, the corner brackets and its layout were drawn for this theme; the icons are Phosphor (MIT, LICENSE-phosphor.txt).
Colors and proportions follow the game's interface as it can be observed in screenshots; the game's textures, logo and icons are not reproduced.

The game's UI fonts are not included. The theme names Eurostile first in its font list, so a machine that has it installed uses it; other machines fall back to Bahnschrift or Segoe UI. Section titles use Old English Text MT (installed with Microsoft Office) when present, then UnifrakturMaguntia or Pirata One, then Bahnschrift.

The XAML files start from Playnite's Default theme (MIT); see LICENSE-Playnite.txt.
