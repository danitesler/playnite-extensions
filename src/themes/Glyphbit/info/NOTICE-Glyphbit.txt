Glyphbit is an unofficial, fan-made Playnite theme inspired by the interface of Hyper Light Drifter.

It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Heart Machine or any rights holder of Hyper Light Drifter.
All names and trademarks belong to their respective owners and appear only to identify the inspiration.

This package contains no game art, logo, font or script from Hyper Light Drifter. Every icon, rune column and
tile mark is original, generated by the theme's own scripts.
