# Tatterspire: creative brief

Mode: **Showcase** (a game). Dark. Notes in `RESEARCH.md`; the reviewed previews are the real spec.

- **Lens (picked):** (a) Diegetic / Artifact: torn paper pinned over a fog-slate world. Dropped (b) HUD: the combat bar and card fan only exist in a fight and would force a "stats strip" theme like Runeskald's tablet. Dropped (c) Title menu: the fleuron list survives as a detail (row and tree selection) but the swash-on-gold-text menu is too thin to carry a whole theme. Freshest against the last five (Runeskald silver tablets, Verst iron riveted, Thornbloom Persian ornament, Rimewash watercolour, Gumshoe chalkboard): none uses torn-edge paper with a slate base and red/gold ribbons.
- **Source DNA:** 1. **Torn edge**: irregular notches, dark outline, lighter inner rim -> menu panel, tooltips, group cards, game-page banner foot, empty page. 2. **Slate fog + torch gold**: cold `--slate` surfaces with warm `--gold` lettering -> every title, selection text. 3. **Slab serif lettering**: bold gold titles, cream body (Zilla Slab) -> headings, buttons. 4. **Hex plates and red ribbons**: Proceed hex, swallow-tail Back flag -> Play, default buttons, close plates, sidebar flags. 5. **Cyan card glow + gold corner brackets**: hover and selection on cards and map nodes -> cover hover, cover selection, dropdown frame.
- **Game element map (10):** attack card (rust frame, silver ribbon, umber well, cost gem) -> grid cover tile (frame + ribbon name plate + gem count); Proceed hex -> Play button; Back red flag -> grid side-panel close and settings close; HUD bar with ragged foot -> top panel; potion capsule -> search box; settings tab (cyan outline) -> `TabControl`; dark-teal checkbox with gold check -> `CheckBox` / `RadioButton`; gold-bracket dropdown + gold arrow -> `ComboBox`; gold hex scroll handle -> `ScrollViewer`; energy orb "3/3" -> `TextBlockGameScore` (hexagon orb); enemy HP bar -> `ProgressBar`; act title band -> game title; map parchment with dotted route and gold-bracket node -> empty game page; legend paper -> tooltips / popups; Continue arrow -> `PrimaryButton`.
- **Signature levers:**
  - Hero banner transition: **torn paper edge**: the banner foot is cut by a ragged page-color strip (sprite `torn-foot.png`, brush-filled through `OpacityMask`).
  - Play button: **Proceed hex** with a lit rim, a gold Y gem on the left, rim sweep on hover, sink on press.
  - Metadata container and group break: **torn index card** per group (GroupBox torn paper, gold slab title), group break is a short gold swash rule.
  - Chip and score: chips are **ribbon tags** (flat tag with a swallow-tail notch); score is the **energy hexagon orb** with the number.
  - Row and cover selection mark: rows get **gold swashes either side** (main menu cursor); covers get **gold corner brackets + cyan glow** (card hover, map node).
  - Side-panel close plate: **red flag ribbon** 28x28 with a swallow tail and an X, `DangerBrush` hover.
  - Menu: torn slate sheet, a **gold swash pair slides out from the label** (no plate), group break is a short gold swash between two thin rules, shortcuts in a **cream rune-tab** (small raised tab) at the right edge, Exit in a red flag; levers not used elsewhere: torn sheet panel, swash cursor, tab-style shortcuts.
- **Hero moment:** Play: the Proceed hex lights its rim (gold sweep left to right, 220 ms), the gold lettering brightens, the gem pulses once; pressed sinks 2 px and drops to `--proceed`.
- **Secondary moments (6):** details row hover/selection (wash + swash slide), cover hover/selection (lift 6 px, cyan outline fades in, gold corners lock on click), cover buttons (hex plates stagger in), sidebar selection (red flag tab unrolls from the left), settings tree selection (gold swash + slate plate), tab (cyan glow), checkbox (gold check draws), slider (gold hex thumb).
- **Motion:** fast 110 ms, base 200 ms, slow 380 ms; `CubicEase` out for entrances, in-out for fades; entrances rise 6 px; stagger 50 ms.
- **Brand:** Tatterspire (echoes "spire"); mark = a twisting tower silhouette with three lit windows and a pennant, drawn from the tower's tapering shape (never the logo); reused in the settings title band, empty page and tile. `GameBannerTintBrush` = `--night`, the darkest slate used on the top bar.
- **Kept calm:** lists, grid backgrounds and the metadata pane are flat slate (no paper texture behind dense content); torn edges only on panels and cards that hold little text.
