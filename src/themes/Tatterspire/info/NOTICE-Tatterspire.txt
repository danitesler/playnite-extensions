Tatterspire is an independent, unofficial Playnite desktop theme by Dani Tesler, inspired by the interface of Slay the Spire 2. Slay the Spire is a trademark of Mega Crit Games. This theme is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Mega Crit Games.

The theme contains no files from the game. Its mark (a twisting tower with three lit windows and a pennant), icons, torn-paper sprites and all other art were drawn or generated for this theme (art/icons.py, art/sprites.py); its colors follow the game's menus as they can be observed in public screenshots, and the game's art, icons, fonts and logo are not reproduced.

Font bundled with the theme, unmodified, in its Typography folder with its license (SIL Open Font License 1.1, OFL.txt):
- Zilla Slab (Copyright 2017, The Mozilla Foundation), titles, buttons and body text.

The XAML files start from Playnite's Default theme (MIT); see LICENSE-Playnite.txt.
