Checkpost is an independent, unofficial Playnite desktop theme by Dani Tesler, inspired by the interface of Papers, Please. Papers, Please and its name are the property of Lucas Pope; this theme is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by him or any publisher.

The theme contains no files from the game. Its name, its mark (a rubber stamp seen from the front over two dotted impressions), its icons, the red seal pip and the permit sheet were drawn for this theme from simple shapes (art/icons.py, XAML vectors). No logo, emblem, ministry seal, country name, character portrait, document or font from the game is reproduced. Its colors follow the game's interface palette as seen in publicly shared screenshots (see RESEARCH.md).

The game's own text faces are not used or bundled.

Fonts bundled with the theme in its Typography folder with their licenses (SIL Open Font License 1.1):
- Zilla Slab (Copyright 2017 The Mozilla Foundation), body text, trimmed to Latin and Cyrillic (OFL-ZillaSlab.txt).
- Pixelify Sans (Copyright 2021 The Pixelify Sans Project Authors), titles, the Play stamp, headings and tabs at 17px and up, trimmed to Latin and Cyrillic; the bundled copy omits the font's f-ligature substitutions (OFL-PixelifySans.txt).

The XAML files start from Playnite's Default theme (MIT); see LICENSE-Playnite.txt.
