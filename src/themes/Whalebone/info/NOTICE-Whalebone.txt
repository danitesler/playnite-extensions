Whalebone

This is an unofficial fan-made theme, inspired by the menus and overlays of Dishonored 2. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Arkane Studios, Bethesda Softworks, ZeniMax Media or Microsoft.

Dishonored is a trademark or registered trademark of ZeniMax Media Inc.

The theme contains no files from the game. Its mark (a medallion ring around a carved bone spindle crossed by a slit bar, with one glass shard breaking through the ring), icons, brush strokes, ink plates, frost frame, marble and bloom sprites were drawn or generated for this theme (art/sprites.py, art/icons.mjs). Its colors follow the game's menus as they can be observed in public screenshots; the game's art, icons, fonts and logo are not reproduced.

Fonts bundled with the theme, unmodified, in its Typography folder with their license (SIL Open Font License 1.1, OFL.txt):
- Zilla Slab (Copyright 2017, The Mozilla Foundation), body text and labels.
- Cinzel (Copyright 2020 The Cinzel Project Authors), titles and buttons.
- Marck Script (Copyright 2011, Denis Masharov and Marck Fogel, Reserved Font Name "Marck Script"), captions and the empty page.

The XAML files start from Playnite's Default theme (MIT); see LICENSE-Playnite.txt.
