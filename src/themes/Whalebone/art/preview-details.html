Description

A plague of salt has crusted the harbour. Slip through the lighthouse quarter after curfew, turn the guard's own clockwork against them and decide who walks out of the Hollow alive.

Every district has three ways in: the rooftops, the sewers, or the front door with a forged writ. The Watchman's Ledger records which you chose. Notes: the third bell tower key is behind the fishmonger.
