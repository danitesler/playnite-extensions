Wildhand is an independent, unofficial Playnite desktop theme by Dani Tesler, inspired by the interface of Balatro. Balatro and its name are the property of LocalThunk and Playstack; this theme is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by them.

The theme contains no files from the game. Its name, its mark (a tilted playing card holding a three-point jester cap), its icons, the four suit shapes and the chip glyph were drawn for this theme from simple shapes (art/icons.py). No logo, card face, joker, cover art, shader, sound or font from the game is reproduced. Its colors follow the game's interface palette as seen in publicly shared screenshots (see RESEARCH.md).

The game's own text face (m6x11 by Daniel Linssen) is not used or bundled.

Fonts bundled with the theme in its Typography folder with their licenses (SIL Open Font License 1.1), trimmed to Latin and Cyrillic:
- Nunito (Copyright 2014 The Nunito Project Authors), body text and every size under 17px (OFL-Nunito.txt).
- Pixelify Sans (Copyright 2021 The Pixelify Sans Project Authors), titles, plate labels, tabs and numerals at 17px and up; the bundled copy omits the font's f-ligature substitutions (OFL-PixelifySans.txt).

The XAML files start from Playnite's Default theme (MIT); see LICENSE-Playnite.txt.
