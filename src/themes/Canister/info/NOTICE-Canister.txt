Canister — Unofficial Fan Theme Notice
======================================

Canister is an unofficial, community-made Playnite Desktop theme inspired by the
visual style of Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles: Mutants Unleashed (2024).

It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or associated with
Nickelodeon, Paramount Global, Viacom Overseas Holdings C.V., Outright Games,
aheartfulofgames, or any related rights holders.

All XAML templates, vector icons, the Canister brand emblem, and procedurally
generated monochrome mask textures in this package are original works created
for this theme. No game files, logos, or copyrighted artwork are included.
