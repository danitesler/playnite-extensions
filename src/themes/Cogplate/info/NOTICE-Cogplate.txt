Cogplate is an unofficial fan theme inspired by the menus, phosphor CRT terminals, purity dossiers, and HUD of Warhammer 40,000: Boltgun 2.
It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Games Workshop, Focus Entertainment, Big Fan Games, or Auroch Digital.
No game assets, logos, or proprietary font files are included.

Playnite Default theme XAML (MIT): see LICENSE-Playnite.txt.
Barlow Condensed and Barlow Semi Condensed fonts (SIL Open Font License 1.1): bundled static TTFs in src/Typography/ (see src/Typography/OFL.txt).
