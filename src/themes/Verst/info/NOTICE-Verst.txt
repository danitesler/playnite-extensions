Verst

This is an unofficial fan-made theme, inspired by the menus of Metro Exodus and Metro: Last Light Redux. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by 4A Games, Deep Silver, PLAION or the Metro novels' author.
Metro, Metro Exodus, Metro: Last Light and their logos are trademarks of their respective owners.

The theme contains no files from either game. Its mark (a tunnel mouth over two rails), icons, sprites and layout were drawn for this theme. Colors and proportions follow the interfaces as they appear in publicly shared screenshots; no textures, fonts, sounds, logos or art are reproduced.

The type is Barlow Condensed and Barlow Semi Condensed (The Barlow Project Authors, SIL Open Font License 1.1) for headings and body text, and Marck Script (Denis Masharov and Marck Fogel, SIL Open Font License 1.1, Reserved Font Name, shipped unmodified) for handwritten notes. All are bundled in src/Typography with their license (OFL.txt). They stand in for the games' own condensed grotesque and handwriting.

The XAML files start from Playnite's Default theme (MIT); see LICENSE-Playnite.txt.
