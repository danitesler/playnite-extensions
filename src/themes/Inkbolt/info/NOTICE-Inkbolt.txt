Inkbolt

Inkbolt is an unofficial fan-made Playnite theme inspired by the interface of Infamous 2. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the game's developer or publisher.

The theme contains no game files, artwork, logos or fonts. Its broken-ring and lightning-bolt mark, icons and sprites were made for this theme.
