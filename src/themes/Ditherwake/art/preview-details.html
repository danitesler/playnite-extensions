EAST INDIA COMPANY · LONDON OFFICE · 14 OCTOBER 1807

In 1802, the merchant barque departed London for the Orient with over two hundred tons of trade goods and sixty souls aboard. Six months later she failed to make her rendezvous at the Cape of Good Hope and was declared lost at sea.

This morning the vessel drifted into Falmouth Anchorage with damaged sails and no crew in sight. Equipped with Henry Evans's ledger and the brass Memento Mortem watch, inspect every deck and prepare a complete assessment of fates.
