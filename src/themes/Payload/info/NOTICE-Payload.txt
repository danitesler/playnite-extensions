Payload

This is an unofficial fan-made theme, inspired by the menus of Overwatch 2. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Blizzard Entertainment.
Overwatch is a trademark of Blizzard Entertainment, Inc.

The theme contains no files from the game or its website. Its mark, the slanted plates and its layout were drawn for this theme; the icons are Google's Material Symbols (Apache License 2.0, LICENSE-material-symbols.txt).
Colors follow the design tokens published in the Overwatch website's stylesheets and the game's interface as it can be observed; no textures or artwork are reproduced.

The game's fonts (BigNoodleTooOblique, Config) are not included. Display headings use Barlow Condensed Bold Italic (by Jeremy Tribby), an open font bundled with the theme; body text uses Config when installed, then Bahnschrift (Windows 10 and later) or Segoe UI. The font files and their license (SIL Open Font License 1.1, OFL.txt) are in the theme's Typography folder.

The XAML files start from Playnite's Default theme (MIT); see LICENSE-Playnite.txt.
