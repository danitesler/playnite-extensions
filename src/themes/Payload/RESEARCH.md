### Design rules observed

- OW2 marks the selected tab or sidebar item with a **full cyan fill and black text**, not an underline.
- The call to action (PLAY, CONTINUE, the logo's "2" tile) is orange ed6516. The site's hovered button is ff7926.
- Display type (page titles, hero names) is BigNoodleTooOblique: uppercase and oblique. Menu and tab labels use bold upright Config.
- Progress (Battle Pass, challenges) is an orange fill on a thin bar.
- Team colors: ally blue 00accf, enemy red d91547.
- Cards: flat fills with 2px corners and no glow. The hero gallery uses 2px light borders.
