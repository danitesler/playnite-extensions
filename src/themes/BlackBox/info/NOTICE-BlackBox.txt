Black Box is an unofficial, fan-made Playnite theme inspired by the YoRHa system menus and HUD of NieR:Automata.

It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Square Enix, PlatinumGames, or any rights holder of NieR:Automata.
All names and trademarks belong to their respective owners and appear only to identify the design inspiration.

This package contains no game art, logos, proprietary fonts, or screen captures from the game.
Every icon, vector ornament, and procedural alpha-mask sprite (src/Images/fx/) is original work generated for this theme.
