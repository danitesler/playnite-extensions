# Joyride: creative brief

Mode: **Showcase** (five games, one era). Dark only. Notes in `RESEARCH.md`; the reviewed previews are the real spec.

- **Lens (picked):** (a) Diegetic / Artifact: **the box-art collage**. The library is a shelf of crime-saga covers: every surface is a painted panel cut by thick black gutters, titles are white Impact with a black outline, and the game page banner is cut into a cover grid with the title in its own box. The pause menu (c) supplies the menus, tabs and selection (white plate, black ink), and the HUD (b) supplies small marks: the money counter for play time, wanted stars for checks and the filter mark, the siren strobe for a running game. Dropped as the main lens: (b) HUD, because a radar and a money counter cannot carry a whole shell and would read as a generic shooter HUD; (c) pause menu alone, because GTA V's flat black and white menu without the art is close to Emberline and Vice Dusk. Freshest against the last five (Brushlight painted smoke, Tatterspire torn slate, Testbed cleanroom aluminium, Rushdown neon crystal, Studlock LEGO plastic): none cuts its surfaces into black-gutter collage panels or prints titles as outlined poster type.
- **Source DNA:** 1. **Black gutter collage grid** -> cover tiles (6px black frame), the game banner (cut into three panels), the empty page, the settings title band, the menu crown. 2. **Pricedown outlined type** (Impact with a black outline and hard shadow) -> game title, Play label, section heads, empty-state title, the settings band title. 3. **Pause menu white plate with black ink** -> selected rows, tabs, tree, sidebar current item, menu highlight. 4. **HUD colors on black**: mission yellow, cash green, wasted red, siren blue, the three character colors -> accent, play time, danger, running, the tri-color tick. 5. **Snappy motion with punch-ins**: highlights jump fast, "mission passed" moments punch in from 110%.
- **Game element map (12):** box-art collage -> game banner grid, cover frame, empty page; title box -> game title plate; pause tabs (white current tab + character color bar) -> `TabControl`, top bar view switches; menu rows (white selected) -> details rows, settings tree, `ComboBox` rows; interaction menu (picture banner header) -> context menu crown; instructional key caps -> menu shortcuts, More/Edit squares; money counter -> play time and scores (Impact digits outlined, cash green); wanted stars -> `CheckBox`, filter mark, checked menu rows, rating; SA health/armor bar -> `ProgressBar`; mission marker corona -> Play hover bloom; "MISSION PASSED" punch-in -> Play press and cover selection; police siren strobe -> running indicator; radar -> side-panel close plate (rounded square like IV/V's radar).
- **Signature levers:**
  - Hero banner transition: **collage cut**: the banner is split into three panels by 6px black gutters (one tall vertical gutter, one horizontal), and its foot ends on a hard black gutter band, not a fade.
  - Play button: **mission slab**: a mission-yellow slab with a 2px black rim and a hard 4px black offset plate, PLAY in Impact with a black outline; hover lights a corona bloom behind it and lifts the slab off its shadow; press slams it onto the shadow.
  - Metadata container and group break: **brief panel**: black 73% panel with a white tab label strip per group (Barlow Condensed caps on a black tab) and a hard gutter rule.
  - Chip and score: chips are **stickers** (black plate, grey rim, 2px hard offset, yellow on hover); scores are **money counters** (Impact digits with a black outline in cash green / mission yellow / wasted red).
  - Row and cover selection mark: rows take the **white pause plate** (black ink) with a mission-yellow left tab; covers take a **yellow gutter frame** that punches in from 104% with a wanted star badge.
  - Side-panel close plate: **radar square**: 28x28 rounded-square black plate with a grey rim and an X, wasted red on hover.
  - Menu: black 92% panel with a **collage crown** (three tiny art panels split by gutters), highlight = **white slab** wiping in from the left over a hard black offset, label to black ink; group break = **black gutter with a tri-color tick** (Michael / Franklin / Trevor) at the icon column; shortcuts = **white instructional key caps**; checked = **wanted star**; Exit = wasted red slab.
- **Hero moment:** Play hover: corona bloom (mission yellow) punches in behind the slab from 80%, the slab rises 2px up-left off its black shadow, the key cap's triangle lights white. Press: the slab slams onto the shadow (90 ms), a white flash sweeps across it.
- **Secondary moments (8):** details row hover (charcoal plate slides in 4px) and selection (white plate wipes in, yellow tab drops in); cover hover (lift 3px, black offset shadow grows) and selection (yellow frame punches in from 104%, star badge pops); cover buttons (white key-cap squares stagger in); sidebar selection (white plate with a yellow foot); tree selection (white plate, yellow tab); tab (white plate with a character color bar); checkbox (star fills gold); slider (yellow block thumb on a segmented bar).
- **Motion:** fast 90 ms, base 180 ms, slow 360 ms; `BackEase` (amplitude 0.3) out for punch-ins, `CubicEase` out for slides; entrances from the left 6px; stagger 50 ms. Ambient: only the empty page (collage panels pan slowly) and the running siren.
- **Brand:** Joyride. Mark = a square collage tile cut by black gutters into three panels with a five-point wanted star in the big one. Used in the sidebar menu button, settings title band, empty page and tile. `GameBannerTintBrush` = `--asphalt-deep`, the blue-black of the night city panels.
- **Kept calm:** library lists and the cover grid sit on flat asphalt; collage art only in the banner, the menu crown, the settings band, the rail's foot and the empty page; outlined poster type only for titles, scores and the empty caption.
