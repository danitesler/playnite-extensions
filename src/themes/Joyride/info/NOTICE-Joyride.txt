Joyride is an independent, unofficial Playnite desktop theme by Dani Tesler, inspired by the interfaces of Grand Theft Auto III, Grand Theft Auto: Vice City, Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas, Grand Theft Auto IV and Grand Theft Auto V. Grand Theft Auto and the names of those games are trademarks of Take-Two Interactive Software; the games are developed and published by Rockstar Games. This theme is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

The theme contains no files from the games. Its name, its mark (a square tile cut into three panels with a five-point star), its icons, the collage scenes (a sunset with palms, a night skyline with a bridge and a helicopter, desert hills with a car), the mission marker bloom, the halftone and all other art were drawn or generated for this theme from simple shapes (art/icons.py, art/sprites.py). No logo, character, cover painting, map, font or other artwork from the games is reproduced. Its colors follow the games' HUD colors as documented by their communities (see RESEARCH.md).

The games' title font (Pricedown) is not used or bundled. Titles use Impact, which ships with Windows and is not bundled.

Fonts bundled with the theme in its Typography folder with their licenses (SIL Open Font License 1.1), trimmed to Latin and Cyrillic:
- Barlow (Copyright 2017 The Barlow Project Authors), body text (OFL-Barlow.txt).
- Barlow Condensed (Copyright 2017 The Barlow Project Authors), captions, tabs and headings (OFL-BarlowCondensed.txt).

The XAML files start from Playnite's Default theme (MIT); see LICENSE-Playnite.txt.
