## Interface Parts & Motion Observations

- **Asymmetric Hand-Cut Geometry**: Save-slot frames pinch inward slightly along all four edges with sharp flared corner points; title banners wave gently with forked swallowtail notches and a small centered chest/shield crest; `Back`/`OK` sits on a curved pointed gold ribbon with a folded darker underside tail.
- **Settings Widgets (`Video`, `Sound`, `Text & Language`)**:
  - Labels sit on the left in warm Pirate Gold (`#E4B217`) serif; controls align cleanly on the right.
  - Checkboxes are bold crisp-white **8-corner notched octagon frames** that fill with a **solid Pirate Gold rotated diamond** when checked.
  - Options use paired Deep Aqua `<` / `>` **wedge chevrons** flanking Burnt Bronze (`#BF620F`) serif values.
- **Pause & Confirm Menus**: Centered vertical serif list over a deep black vignette flanked by translucent dark-aqua theater curtains and golden corner totems. Group breaks use a **tapered golden hairline centered on a small geometric pirate skull**.
- **Inventory Pouch & Contextual Prompts**: Espresso-leather scalloped satchel with a dashed inner stitch seam; floating dark translucent charcoal pill (`Open my todo list`, `Feed the parrot`, `Shipyard`) paired with a crisp **4-tick X crosshair cursor**.
- **Achievements & Scrapbook**: Notched square badge plaques with percentages, a crisp **white winged-flourish colophon** at bottom center, and chamfered Trivia-card answer plates with emerald/red rims.
- **Motion Feel**: Theatrical storybook cut-paper craft — quick crisp reveals (`110–140ms`) with a gentle playful overshoot (`BackEase` amplitude `0.24` on primary ribbon/button pops and `CubicEase` Out `160–220ms` for calm panel washes, curtain reveals, and row highlights). Zero harsh digital neon flicker; instead warm lantern glints, drifting ghost-ship wisps while launching/running, and ribbon/cursor slides.
