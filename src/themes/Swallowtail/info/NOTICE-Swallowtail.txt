Swallowtail — Unofficial Fan Theme Notice & Third-Party Licenses

Swallowtail is an unofficial, fan-made desktop theme for Playnite inspired by
the nocturnal Caribbean theater menus and storybook craft of Return to Monkey
Island (2022). It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Terrible
Toybox, Devolver Digital, or Lucasfilm Ltd. All vector marks, icons, textures,
and XAML templates are original works; no game assets, logos, or trademarks are
included.
