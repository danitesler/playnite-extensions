CS 1.6

This is an unofficial fan-made theme, inspired by the menus of Counter-Strike 1.6 and the Steam client skin of the same era. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Valve Corporation.
Counter-Strike, Half-Life, Steam and the Valve logo are trademarks and/or registered trademarks of Valve Corporation.

The theme contains no files from the game or from Steam. Its mark, icons and layout were drawn for this theme. Colors and proportions follow the interface as it is described by the publicly distributed scheme files (ClientScheme.res); no textures, fonts, sounds, logos or maps are reproduced.

The type is Verdana, which ships with Windows and is not bundled.

The XAML files start from Playnite's Default theme (MIT); see LICENSE-Playnite.txt.
