## Typography Analysis

- **Source faces**: Condensed industrial/arcade sans-serif (`FIGHTING GROUND`, `Options`, `Battle Settings`, `LUKE`, `MANON`) with subtle hollow/neon stroke energy on major headers and octagonal match numerals (`78`, `3`); clean humanist/neo-grotesque sans-serif for dense settings rows, descriptions, and metadata; tabular monospace for numeric readouts and controller prompt keys.
- **Bundled open stand-ins (`src/Typography/`)**:
  - **Body & UI (`FontFamily`)**: **Barlow** (`Regular`, `Medium`, `SemiBold`, `Bold`; SIL OFL 1.1) with `Segoe UI` fallback — crisp neutral neo-grotesk matching Options rows, descriptions, and metadata tables.
  - **Headings & CTAs (`HeadingFontFamily`)**: **Rajdhani** (`SemiBold`, `Bold`; SIL OFL 1.1) with `Segoe UI` fallback — squared, condensed industrial-arcade display proportions matching `Options`, `FIGHTING GROUND`, `LUKE`, and tournament banners.
  - **Shortcuts & Monospace (`MonospaceFontFamily`)**: `Consolas` (Windows native; drawn via `Cascadia Mono` in previews).

## Interface Parts & Motion

- **45° Top-Right Chamfered Capsules & Roster Tiles**: Dark slate-indigo plates clipped at 45° on the top-right corner with crisp steel borders.
- **Prismatic Faceted White-Ice Selection Bar**: Active Options row & Character Select tile snap to a luminous white-ice crystal fill textured with diagonal low-poly facet triangles and high-contrast `#0E1220` ink.
- **6-Segment Slanted Drive Gauge**: Slanted parallelogram energy cells below the match health bars (`Drive Gauge`) — recreated via `LocalDriveSegmentsMaskBrush` for `ProgressBar` and `RunningScan` sweeps.
- **Diagonal Header Ribbons & Diamond-Capped Technical Rules**: World Tour cards (`Safety Pin`, `Black Dye`) feature 45°-tipped cyan header ribbons, while two-column Options screens separate panes with thin rules terminated by square/diamond endpoints.
- **Top-Center V-Notch Modal Crown**: The `Information` modal's neon-magenta header rule dips into a centered downward V-notch pointer into the panel body.
- **Motion personality**: Crisp arcade-input snap (`CubicEase` / `BackEase` `0.18–0.22` on cursor/badge lock-ins, `110–140ms` entrances, `180–240ms` exits with `FillBehavior="Stop"`), slanted crystal sheen flashes on `PlayButton` hover, and slanted 6-segment Drive Gauge sweeps (`1.6s` cycle) while launching.
