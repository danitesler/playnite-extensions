Hallowtide is an unofficial, fan-made Playnite theme. It is inspired by the quest book interface of the community game
"All Hallows: The Land of Lost Dreams" (made in Dreams). It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the
creators of that game, Media Molecule or Sony Interactive Entertainment. "Dreams" and "Made in Dreams" belong to
their owners and are named here only to say what inspired the look. Nothing from the game is included: no artwork,
logo, audio or font. All ornaments (rope frame, torn and ink plates, thorn rule, spirals, serpent ribbon, motes,
ink blot) are original shapes generated by the script art/sprites.py in the project repository, the icons are original
stroked glyphs drawn in Media.xaml, and the colours are values sampled from public screenshots.

Playnite's Default theme files are the base of the XAML (MIT, see LICENSE-Playnite.txt).

Bundled fonts (src/Typography, SIL Open Font License 1.1, licenses in OFL.txt):
  Cinzel (SemiBold, Bold)                      The Cinzel Project Authors
  Gelasio (Regular, Bold)                      The Gelasio Project Authors
  Barlow Semi Condensed (Medium, SemiBold)     The Barlow Project Authors
  Patrick Hand (Regular)                       Patrick Wagesreiter
The fonts are cut into static instances and trimmed to Latin and Cyrillic where the font carries them.
