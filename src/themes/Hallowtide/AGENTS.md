# Hallowtide — theme notes

## What this is

Playnite **Desktop** theme (`ThemeApiVersion` 2.9.0, loads on Playnite 10.45+), key `hallowtide`, folder `Hallowtide`, `AddonId` `Hallowtide_015DA7E0`. A dark plum-black shell with the game's inked storybook drawn over it: parchment pages, hand-inked rope borders, torn paper, ink-brush plates, wax pins, bookmark ribbons, a serpent-headed page header, and the warm lantern light of its scenes.

**Unofficial fan theme**: inspired by the quest book UI of *All Hallows: The Land of Lost Dreams* (a community game made in Media Molecule's Dreams). Not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the game's creators, Media Molecule or Sony. "Hallowtide" is its own name. No game asset, logo or font is shipped: every ornament is generated by `art/sprites.py`, the icons are drawn in `src/Media.xaml`, the colours are sampled values in `src/tokens.css`. See `info/NOTICE-Hallowtide.txt`.

Shared anatomy (file map, shell rules, game page skeleton, metadata pane, build and first-run checks): **`../AGENTS.md`**. Loading rules and build checks: **`.claude/skills/playnite-theme-dev/reference.md`**.

## Ornaments

`art/sprites.py` (deterministic, Pillow and numpy) writes the white masks in `src/Images/fx/` at 2 px per DIP and a contact sheet `art/fx-sheet.png`: `rope-frame` (9-slice 36+48+36), `plate-torn` (20+32+20), `plate-ink` (16+32+16, drawn at 0.75), `glow-frame` (44+40+44), `thorn-tile`, `spiral-pair`, `spiral-mark`, `serpent-ribbon`, `mote`, `ink-blot`. `Common.xaml` turns them into the templates (`InkFrameTemplate`, `TornPlateTemplate`, `InkPlateTemplate`, `GlowFrameTemplate`, `DividerTemplate`, `SerpentRibbonTemplate`, `PinTemplate`, `BookmarkTemplate`, `SteamScreenshotsSkeletonTemplate`). The solid plates carry an interior underlay so the 9-slice cell seams never show. `art/fonts.py` cuts the bundled faces. `art/gen-overviews.mjs` writes both game pages and applies the repo's breakpoints; `art/build-previews.mjs` writes both previews (icons read from `Media.xaml`).

| `Views/Library.xaml`, `Views/LibraryGridView.xaml` | Library game art only while Playnite's window background setting is on, at full strength with Playnite's own darken and blur (repo rule "Window background"); grid width cap kept |

**Settings chrome** (`DefaultControls/TreeView.xaml`, `DerivedStyles/StandardWindowStyle.xaml`, `WindowBarButton.xaml`): the section list is the book's contents list: 30px rows in the body face, hover a brown ink plate with a half-lit spiral, selected the indigo plate with a lantern bar and a turning lantern spiral (two cues beyond colour). The title band is 44px: the **serpent-headed ribbon** ghosted into the right end, a lantern spiral and the title in carved caps, closed by the thorn rule that hangs 7px below.

## Game page

Follows the skeleton in `../AGENTS.md` (generated by `art/gen-overviews.mjs`). The open book: a dark page with the toned banner, the title in Cinzel Bold with the thorn rule under it, then two parchment pages: description and notes on the left, every metadata field on the right (six groups split by the thorn rule drawn in each group's top padding, so the first group's rule is clipped away). A page is paper, a burnt edge and the inked rope frame; text on a page is ink (`Parchment*` brushes) and chips are ink plates. Below 400px of main width the metadata page stacks under the description in two columns at 560px of usable width, one below.

Empty state: an open book with a lantern bloom and a wand spark that drifts and breathes (a loop that runs only while it shows) and `LOCErrorNoGameSelected` under the thorn rule.

## Deviations

- Menu icons Playnite copies (`AddGameIcon`, ...) keep Playnite's icofont glyphs; the rest of the icons are the theme's own inked strokes.
- Popups cannot cast shadows (layered windows), so the dialogue box and the pinned note rely on their edge and plate shape.
- The book's drip-edged place labels and the wooden "You are here" sign are not drawn: nothing in Playnite maps to them.
- Motion is inferred from stills (RESEARCH.md, Motion); timings are this theme's own.

## Previews

`art/preview-details.html` and `art/preview-settings.html` are generated by `art/build-previews.mjs` from the tokens, `Media.xaml` icon geometry and the same sprites. Motifs drawn: the red bookmark ribbon and blue tab on the rail, the thorn rule closing the top bar, ink-plate list rows (one hovered, one indigo with the wand spark), the torn Play plate with its lantern bloom and fireflies, the open-book parchment pages with the inked rope frame, ink chips, the serpent-ribbon title band, the contents-list settings tree with the spiral mark, the ticked slider with the diamond thumb. Stand-in fonts: none needed, all four faces are bundled. Footer note: unofficial, not affiliated.
