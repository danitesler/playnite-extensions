Counterfoil — Unofficial Fan Theme Notice

Counterfoil is an original, unofficial Playnite Desktop theme inspired by the visual
language of Disco Elysium and analog 35mm FELD film playback equipment. It is not
affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by ZA/UM.

All XAML templates, vector icon geometries, procedural sprite masks (art/sprites.py),
and preview artwork are original creations for this repository. No game assets,
textures, logos, or trademarks are redistributed.
