Vesperglass is an unofficial fan theme inspired by the deep plum twilight glass, burnished rose-copper astrolabe framing, electric amethyst star flares, and lyrium crystal accents of Dragon Age: The Veilguard.
It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Electronic Arts Inc. or BioWare.
No game assets, official logos, copyrighted artwork, or proprietary font files are included.
