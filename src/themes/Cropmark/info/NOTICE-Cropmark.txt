Cropmark — Unofficial Fan Theme Notice

Cropmark is an original, unofficial Playnite Desktop theme inspired by the comic-book
illustration Bristol board aesthetic, non-photo blue registration marks, and menu design
of Marvel Tokon: Fighting Souls.

This theme is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or associated with Marvel,
Arc System Works Co., Ltd., or Sony Interactive Entertainment Inc. It contains no official
game artwork, logos, trademarks, or proprietary fonts.
