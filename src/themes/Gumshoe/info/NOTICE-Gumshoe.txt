Gumshoe

This is an unofficial fan-made theme, inspired by the menus and HUD of Mouse: P.I. For Hire. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Fumi Games or the game's publisher.
Mouse: P.I. For Hire and its logo are trademarks of their respective owners.

The theme contains no files from the game. Its mark, icons, sprites and layout were drawn for this theme. Colors and proportions follow the interface as it appears in publicly shared screenshots; no textures, fonts, sounds, logos or art are reproduced.

The type is Patrick Hand (Patrick Wagesreiter, SIL Open Font License 1.1) for body text and headings, and Anton (The Anton Project Authors, SIL Open Font License 1.1) for poster capitals. Both are bundled in src/Typography with their license (OFL.txt). They stand in for the game's own hand-lettered faces.

The XAML files start from Playnite's Default theme (MIT); see LICENSE-Playnite.txt.
