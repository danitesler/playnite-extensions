# Clearwater — theme notes

## What this is

Playnite **Desktop** theme (`ThemeApiVersion` 2.9.0) inspired by **macOS 26 (Tahoe) Liquid Glass** (light variant).
Unofficial fan theme; not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Apple Inc. See [`RESEARCH.md`](RESEARCH.md), [`BRIEF.md`](BRIEF.md), and `info/NOTICE-Clearwater.txt`.

Shared anatomy: **`../AGENTS.md`**. Loading rules and build checks: **`docs/themes/reference.md`**.

## Tokens & Typography

- Tokens live in `src/tokens.css` (`--tahoe-*`) and map to Playnite/shared keys in `src/Constants.template.xaml`.
- Typography: bundled `Barlow` (`Regular`, `Medium`, `SemiBold`, `Bold`, SIL OFL) in `src/Typography/`, subset to Latin + Cyrillic, referenced as `./Typography/#Barlow, Barlow, Segoe UI`.
- Radii: `ControlCornerRadius` = `8px`, `CornerRadiusSmall` = `6px`, `CornerRadiusLarge` = `12px`, `CornerRadiusXLarge` = `16px`.

## Shell & Signature Elements

- **Sidebar (`Views/Sidebar.xaml`, `CustomControls/SidebarItem.xaml`)**: 44px floating Liquid Glass rail with crisp white specular rim, crowned by `PART_ElemMainMenu` hosting `LocalClearwaterMenuMarkTemplate` (a custom Liquid Glass Prism Lens whose outer ring scales and specular arc lifts on hover, and whose optical lens fills System Blue with a breathing core while open). Active items wear a System Blue optical pill (`#007AFF`).
- **Top bar (`Views/TopPanel.xaml`, `CustomControls/TopPanelItem.xaml`, `CustomControls/SearchBox.xaml`)**: 48px translucent Liquid Glass toolbar with Spotlight search pill (persistent `Glyph`), System Orange dot filter mark (`TextFilter`), sorting-first tools, icon-only view switches, 12px optical blue `PART_ProgressGlobal`, and 108px right clearance for traffic-light window buttons.
- **Traffic-light window controls (`DerivedStyles/MainWindowStyle.xaml`, `StandardWindowStyle.xaml`, `WindowBarButton.xaml`)**: `#FEBC2E` Minimize, `#28C840` Maximize/Restore, and `#FF5F57` Close circular dots revealing crisp dark symbols on hover.
- **Menus (`DefaultControls/ContextMenu.xaml`, `Menu.xaml`, `ComboBox.xaml`)**: Unified `LocalClearwaterMenuBoardTemplate` (12px frosted glass card with crisp white top specular rim and `glass-rim.png` bloom), 30px rows, System Blue highlight capsule, 24 full-color 48×48 Liquid Glass squircle PNG icons (`art/icons.py` → `src/Images/MacGlass/*.png`), SF checkmarks, and System Red `Alt+Q` Exit row.
- **Game pages (`Views/DetailsViewGameOverview.xaml`, `Views/GridViewGameOverview.xaml`, generated by `art/gen-overviews.mjs`)**: Proportional `MathConverter` banner spacer, Liquid Glass Blue Lens `PlayButton` with diagonal `glass-sheen.png` hover sweep, System Green `RunningIndicator` with 3 breathing glass pips, macOS Get Info frosted glass metadata sheet (300px usable inner width, `GameDetailsPanePosition` Left / Center / Stretch / Right), frosted glass `EmptyGameTemplate`, and custom 28×28 Liquid Glass `SidePanelCloseButton`.
