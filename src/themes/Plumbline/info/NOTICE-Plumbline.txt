Plumbline is an unofficial, fan-made Playnite theme inspired by the menu interface of The Sims 4.

It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Electronic Arts Inc., Maxis, or any rights holder of The Sims.
All names and trademarks belong to their respective owners and appear only to identify the inspiration.

This package contains no game art, logo, font or screen capture from the game. Colours were measured from public
screenshots; every shape (the facets, discs, capsules, needs bars, the plumb-bob mark and the icon set) is original and
drawn in XAML or generated by scripts in the repository. The mark is a plumb bob on a cord: it is not the game's
plumbob.

Text is set in Segoe UI, a font Windows provides. Nothing is bundled.
