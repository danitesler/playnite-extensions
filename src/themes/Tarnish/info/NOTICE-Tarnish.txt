Tarnish is an unofficial, fan-made Playnite theme inspired by the interface of Elden Ring.

It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by FromSoftware, Bandai Namco Entertainment or any rights holder of
Elden Ring. All names and trademarks belong to their respective owners and appear only to identify the inspiration.

This package contains no game art, logo, rune, font or screen capture from Elden Ring. Every icon, shape, glow
sprite and the tile mark is original, drawn or generated by the theme's own scripts.

Headings use Cinzel (by Natanael Gama), an open font bundled with the theme. The font file and its license
(SIL Open Font License 1.1, OFL.txt) are in the theme's Typography folder.
