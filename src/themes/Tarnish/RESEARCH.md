## Sampling method

Each capture was drawn into a 1920x1080 canvas in the browser and read with `getImageData`. Fills use the region median; text and light use the mean of the brightest 2% of the region (anti-aliasing and JPEG noise make single pixels unreliable). Vertical and horizontal pixel walks located edges. Coordinates are in the 1920x1080 image.

## Interface rules read from the captures

- **Everything fades.** Panels, bars and rules have no hard ends: the selected option is an olive bar that fades out on both sides, rules are hairlines that are brightest in the middle or at one end. Hard rectangles are reserved for buttons, item slots and icon tiles.
- **Selection is light, not colour.** The selected row is the same grey-white text on a lit bar with small left and right arrowheads. Gold is rare: the selected inventory slot, the title logotype and big-moment banners.
- **Buttons** (Key Bindings, Next, OK) are black plates in a 1px light outline with the label centred; no fill colour until selected.
- **Headers**: an icon tile, then the screen name in plain serif, on a black band that fades out to the right.
- **Icon tabs** (System, Inventory): a row of engraved icons with no frames; the current one sits on a patch of light with a short curved underline.
- **Sliders**: a hairline track with end ticks and a small bright bar as the marker; the value is a number to the right.
- **Status panel** (Inventory, right): label left, value right-aligned, groups separated by empty space, section headers with a small icon and a full-width hairline.
- **Light streak**: "Press any button" sits on a horizontal lens streak (hot hairline core, flat elliptical bloom). The same light appears under "Enemy Felled" style banners.
- **Type**: old-style serif at about 22px on 1080p for options (about 14px at 100% desktop scale), capitals with wide tracking only for banners and the logotype.

## Motion

The database holds stills plus short video clips; durations below are estimates from those clips and from memory of the game, not frame-stepped measurements.

- Menu selection moves instantly, but the bar itself has a slow soft pulse while it rests.
- Banners ("Enemy Felled", area names) fade in over roughly half a second with the gold streak widening under the text, hold, and fade out.
- Sites of grace emit a slow, breathing golden light with a thin ray; nothing in the UI snaps or bounces.

What the theme takes from this: fades of 0.12-0.35s with no overshoot on chrome, slow breathing loops (2.2-3.6s) only on the thing under the pointer or on the empty page, and the gold streak as the one shared light effect (Play, buttons, focus, the current tab and rail item). The cover buttons keep the repo's standard rise-and-settle entrance.

## Not taken from the game

No logo, rune, ring mark, font, texture, screenshot or icon. The tile mark and the empty-page figure are an original four-point glint over a ring of light on the ground. The name only echoes "the Tarnished".
