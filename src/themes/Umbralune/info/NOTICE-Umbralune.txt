Umbralune — Unofficial Playnite Desktop Theme

Umbralune is an original, unofficial fan-made Playnite desktop theme inspired by the
visual design language of Bayonetta 2. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or
sponsored by PlatinumGames Inc., SEGA, or Nintendo. All artwork, vector marks, icon
sets, and procedural texture masks shipped with this theme are original works.
