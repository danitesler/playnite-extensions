Libertalia: notices

This is an unofficial fan theme, inspired by the look of the menus of Uncharted 4: A Thief's End. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Naughty Dog, Sony Interactive Entertainment or PlayStation.
Uncharted is a trademark of Sony Interactive Entertainment LLC; it is mentioned only to say what the look of this theme takes inspiration from.

No logos, icons, fonts, images or other game files are included. The colors and sizes were sampled from published screenshots of the menus and written down as plain values. The compass mark is original. The UI font is Constantia, which ships with Windows; the game's own typeface is not bundled.
