Description

Navigate four interconnected boroughs across rain-slicked expressways, steel suspension spans, and late-night transit depots. Every district carries its own grid rhythm — from tight Midtown avenues and Middle Park drives to industrial Alderney docks and sweeping Broker interchanges.

Overpass translates the stark monochrome discipline of late-2000s urban cartography into a calm, high-contrast library shell: layered procedural street maps dissolve naturally into pitch-black water behind sparse surfaces while keeping lists and prose crisp and distraction-free.
