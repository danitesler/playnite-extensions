Overpass — Unofficial Fan Theme Notice

Overpass is an unofficial community-created Playnite Desktop theme inspired by the minimalist monochrome pause menu and cartographic map aesthetic of Grand Theft Auto IV.

- Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Rockstar Games, Rockstar North, or Take-Two Interactive Software, Inc.
- Contains zero proprietary game artwork, map exports, icons, logos, audio, or commercial fonts from Grand Theft Auto IV. All cartographic masks (art/sprites.py), vector icons (src/Media.xaml), menu icons (art/menu-icons.json), and the Overpass emblem (art/mark.svg) are original works created for this theme.
