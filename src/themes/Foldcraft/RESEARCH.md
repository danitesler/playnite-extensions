## Interface Parts & Motion Observations

- **Cut & Folded Geometry:** Every panel reads as layered construction paper or cardstock: **serrated postage-stamp edges** (`1/40`), **accordion-folded 3×2 creased letter sheets** (`14/40`, `18/40`), **deckled torn charcoal prompt strips** (`36/40`, `39/40`), **radial fan bookmark tabs** (`1/40`, `3/40`, `15/40`, `26/40`, `28/40`), **blue dog-ear folded photo corners** (`28/40`), and **concentric circular paper cutouts** (`19/40`, `30/40`).
- **Shadows & Depth:** Short, crisp bottom-right offset studio paper shadows (`2–4px` offsets in warm umber/cardstock shadow `#C8BFAA` / `#181615`, never soft digital drop-blur).
- **Controls & Widgets:**
  - **Navigation Fan:** Colored rectangular tabs jutting cleanly from the left edge; active item slides out smoothly and swaps to crisp contrasting paper (`#F7F5EE` or `#95C5B0`).
  - **Toggles (`3/40`):** Dual-half folded paper controls pairing Emerald `[✓]` ON with Crimson `[✕]` OFF; option tiles (`4/40`) pop up, tilt ~3–5°, and switch from flat grey to vibrant emerald.
  - **Spinners & Counters (`18/40`, `30/40`, `35/40`):** Tangerine bar with triangular up/down steppers; curled sky-blue scroll ribbon with bright gold counters; wavy postal cancellation ink lines across 100%-complete items (`16/40`).
- **Motion Feel:** Tactile stop-motion papercraft — crisp `110–140ms` slides and `BackEase` spring pops (`~180–210ms`) that give paper tabs, cover mounts, and tiles physical weight without sloppy digital glow.
