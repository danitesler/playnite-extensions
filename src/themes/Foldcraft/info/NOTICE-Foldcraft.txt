Foldcraft — Unofficial Fan Theme Notice

Foldcraft is an original, unofficial Playnite Desktop theme inspired by the tactile
papercraft menus, self-healing cutting mats, accordion-creased cardstock dialogs, and
handmade construction-paper UI of Tearaway (2013).

This theme is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or associated with
Media Molecule Ltd. or Sony Interactive Entertainment. It contains no official game
artwork, characters, logos, trademarks, or proprietary fonts.

Original Assets & Open Fonts
- Custom vector Origami Seal brand mark (art/mark.svg), bespoke cut-paper UI icon
  geometries (src/Media.xaml), and deterministic white-mask cutting-mat, paper-fiber,
  deckled-edge, blueprint-fold, sheen, and confetti sprites (art/sprites.py) are original
  works created for Foldcraft.
