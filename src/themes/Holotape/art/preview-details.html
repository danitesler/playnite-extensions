Description

A lone courier picks up a looping signal from an abandoned relay in the hills above the old highway. Trace it across a sun-bleached valley, barter with the settlers who live under the transmitter, and decide who gets to own the airwaves once the music comes back on.

Open-ended quests, a slow day and night cycle and a radio that remembers every station you have tuned.
