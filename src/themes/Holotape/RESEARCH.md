# Holotape — research & sources

Reference: the **Fallout 3** (2008) Pip-Boy 3000 / HUD and the **Fallout: New Vegas** (2010) Pip-Boy and RobCo terminal screens. There is no published design system and no token package, so everything below comes from the two sources named per row. Where a value was not measured it says so; nothing here was sampled from a screenshot or a game capture.

## Design rules taken from the references

| From | Kept | Dropped |
|------|------|---------|
| New Vegas | Warm sepia / yellow-brown backdrops (near-black here), the amber `#FFB642` for titles and section headers, parchment-tinted body text | Full-screen amber monochrome |
| Fallout 3 | The Pip-Boy green `#1AFF80` as the **only** emphasis: selection, focus, checked marks, Play, progress | Green-on-black everywhere, heavy glow and bloom |
| Both | 1px outlined selection box, header rules with short end ticks, segmented stat bars, the "equipped" filled square, square corners, dimmed unselected rows, faint scanlines | Vault Boy art, curved CRT, flicker, noise, all-caps monospace body text |

Body text stays proportional (Bahnschrift, Segoe UI): a monospace description reads as a gimmick within a page. Monofonto is used for headings, titles and the Play label only.

## Preview rendering

- Previews are drawn from the XAML values and `src/tokens.css`, not from game captures. The banner is an invented scene (a lattice radio tower over mesas), built from token colors, with the faint scanline mask the XAML draws.
- Fonts: previews render with bundled open fonts. Monofonto and Fixedsys Excelsior 3.01 are aliased to Cascadia Mono (OFL) in `scripts/data/fonts.json`; Bahnschrift to Barlow, Bahnschrift SemiCondensed to Barlow Semi Condensed, Segoe UI to Selawik. Real Monofonto is narrower and has its own letterforms, so heading widths in a real install differ.

## Gaps

- No captures of either game were measured; row heights, gaps and the amber / green mix are judged, not sampled.
- The exact Fallout terminal font is unverified.
- The sample game, studio and descriptions in the previews are fictional.
