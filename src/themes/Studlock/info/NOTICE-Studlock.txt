Studlock — Unofficial Fan Theme Notice

Studlock is an original, unofficial Playnite Desktop theme inspired by the molded
charcoal baseplate, chamfered cerulean brick frames, studded violet header plaques,
canary yellow focus bars, and Technic axle controls of LEGO Party! (2025).

This theme is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or associated with
The LEGO Group, SMG Studio, or Fictions. It contains no official game artwork,
logos, trademarks, minifigures, or proprietary fonts.
