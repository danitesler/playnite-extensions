Gilded Rift

This is an unofficial fan-made theme, inspired by the League of Legends client and League of Legends: Wild Rift. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Riot Games.
League of Legends, Wild Rift, Hextech and their logos are trademarks of their respective owners.

The theme contains no files from either game. Its mark, icons, sprites and layout were drawn for this theme. Colors and proportions follow the interface as it appears in publicly shared screenshots; no textures, fonts, sounds, logos or art are reproduced.

The type is Cinzel (Natanael Gama, SIL Open Font License 1.1) for headings and Segoe UI (shipped with Windows) for body text. Cinzel is bundled in src/Typography with its license (OFL.txt). They stand in for the games' own faces, which are proprietary.

The XAML files start from Playnite's Default theme (MIT); see LICENSE-Playnite.txt.
