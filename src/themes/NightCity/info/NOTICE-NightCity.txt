Night City

This is an unofficial fan-made theme, inspired by the yellow menus of Cyberpunk 2077. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by CD PROJEKT RED or CD PROJEKT S.A.
Cyberpunk and Cyberpunk 2077 are registered trademarks of CD PROJEKT S.A.; Cyberpunk is a trademark of R. Talsorian Games.

The theme contains no files from the game. Its mark, chamfered plates and layout were drawn for this theme; the icons are Phosphor (MIT, LICENSE-phosphor.txt).
The yellow is the franchise and fan-UI yellow; the other colors and proportions follow the game's interface as it can be observed.

The game's UI fonts (Rajdhani, Orbitron) are open-source (SIL OFL) but are not included, because Playnite themes cannot ship fonts. The theme names Rajdhani first in its font list, so a machine that has it installed uses it; other machines fall back to Bahnschrift (Windows 10/11) or Segoe UI.

The XAML files start from Playnite's Default theme (MIT); see LICENSE-Playnite.txt.
