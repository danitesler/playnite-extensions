# NightCity — research & sources

## Sources

| What | Where |
|------|-------|
| Yellow as the identity color | CDPR lead environment artist Michał Janiszewski on choosing yellow over the genre's red/blue (wnhub.io/news/marketing/item-4676, gameworldobserver.com 2022-05-25). |
| Palette | Brand yellow `#FCEE0A` (commonly cited for the logo; also seen as `#FCE205` in fan breakdowns), neon cyan and "hot" red `#FF003C` as accents on black (claudskills.com cyberpunk-ui skill, a summary of the game's UI conventions). Panel greys, dim yellow edges and greens sampled by eye from main menu, inventory and settings captures; treat them as approximate. |
| Fonts | Rajdhani (Regular/Medium/SemiBold/Bold) for the whole in-game UI, Orbitron for minor labels (fontsinuse.com/uses/60926/cyberpunk-2077-video-game). |
| Shape language | Clipped corners instead of rounded ones, thin double rules, corner accent triangles (same summaries, plus captures). |
| Icons | Phosphor 2.1.1 regular, MIT (`info/LICENSE-phosphor.txt`), generated by `art/phosphor.py` |
| Add-on tile | `art/mark.svg`, original: a chamfered plate with a slash |
| Structure | Part names, triggers and the game-page skeleton were carried over from Playnite's Default files and restyled. |
