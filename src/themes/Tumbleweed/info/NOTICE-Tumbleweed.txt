Tumbleweed

This is an unofficial fan-made theme, inspired by the menus of Red Dead Redemption. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.
Red Dead Redemption is a trademark of Rockstar Games.

The theme contains no files from the game. Its mark, the paint splash, the torn ends of the Play bar and its layout were drawn for this theme; the icons are Phosphor (MIT, LICENSE-phosphor.txt).
Colors and proportions follow the game's interface as it can be observed; the game's textures are not reproduced.

The game's title font (Chinese Rocks, Typodermic) is licensed for desktop use only and is not included. The theme bundles open stand-ins instead: Anton (by Vernon Adams) for titles and Oswald Light (by Vernon Adams and contributors) for body text. The font files and their license (SIL Open Font License 1.1, OFL.txt) are in the theme's Typography folder.

The XAML files start from Playnite's Default theme (MIT); see LICENSE-Playnite.txt.
