# Code of Conduct

Be direct and respectful. No harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks.
This is a solo-maintained project; review time is limited and focused,
small PRs get attention first.

Unacceptable behavior can be reported via a private vulnerability report
(see SECURITY.md) or by contacting the maintainer through the profile
at https://danitesler.com. Violations may lead to removal from
issues, PRs, and discussions.
